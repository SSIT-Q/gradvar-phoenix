## Should-fix

**S1. Holm rationale for the unflagged points.**

- *Location.* Section 5 (ii) bullet 2; the `build_family` docstring.
- *Problem.* "Leaving out its SE_cal can only lower its p, so it never helps a flag in the step-down" has the direction backwards.
  Holm is monotone: lowering any p can only lower or keep every adjusted p. Omitting SE_cal for the unflagged points can therefore
  only help a flag.
- *Why it is inert today.*
  - Unflagged points: |z|/φ ≤ 2.07/1.42 = 1.46, so p ≥ 0.145.
  - Level-1 twins: p = 0.023 (n19) and 0.048 (n85).
  - All of these rank after any test that can reach step 6 (|z_r| > 3, so p < 0.0027), and after both recorded flags.
- *Fix.* Either give every grid point its full (i) z, or replace the sentence with this rank argument.

**S2. SE_cal "bound" wording.**

- *Location.* 1a (i); Section 5 (i) item 3; the `calibration_rel_se` docstring.
- *Problem.* Taking the largest drift over the four classes bounds the first-order drift for any split among classes. Within a class,
  however, the unweighted placement mean only stands in for the light-cone-weighted rate, and it can understate that rate's drift when
  a few locations dominate.
- *Fix.* Write "bounds across classes; within a class the placement mean is a proxy".

**S3. Conformal wording and precision.**

- *Location.* 1a (i), "split-conformal factor"; Section 9, last bullet.
- *Problem 1.* The split-conformal guarantee holds at the 68 % level. The 3σ bar, 3φ, assumes a normal shape.
- *Problem 2.* The 19 scores are 8 same-draw level pairs (correlation 0.995) plus 3 single points, so about 11 are independent.
  - With calibrated N(0, 1) scores in this structure, φ ≥ 1.42 occurs in about 12 % of simulations, and the 90 % range of φ is
    1.00 to 1.60.
  - Collapsing each pair to one score gives φ = 1.43, so the value stands; its precision is low.
- *Fix.* Say both things in the row.

**S4. κ_e at n = 19.**

- *Location.* 1a "Effect on the recorded flags", last sentence; Section 7 "Reading" bullets 3 and 4.
- *Problem.* κ_e = 4.8 is computed from the population ratio R = 0.600. The draw factor D = 0.653 accounts for 84 % of ln R.
  - The device part on the draws that ran, b²/F_pred = 0.913, corresponds to κ_e = 1.67 [1.25, 2.07]. This uses the b² interval
    without the calibration term; with A instead of b² it is 1.62.
  - 1.67 lies inside the layered-over-isolated range (1.4 to 1.7) and inside κ̂_e's interval.
- *Fix.* Write:

  > A uniform per-layer excess cannot produce either flag's population ratio; at n = 19 the device part of the deficit (draw-matched,
  > (iv)) corresponds to κ_e ≈ 1.7 [1.2, 2.1], consistent with a layered-over-isolated excess.

  Where (iv) exists, also report κ_e from b²/F_pred.
