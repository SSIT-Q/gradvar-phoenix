# Theorist-referee pass (delegated; for the college theorist's countersignature)

Materials: prereg Q1 v0.9.8, P2 v0.4.1, Gate 1 report, main @ 1549a12, prior reviews (not repeated). Checks in `scratchpad/review/theorist/q1_check.py` (independent, no project code). Mele et al. cited by arXiv numbering (Corollary 8 / Theorem 10 = Nature Physics Corollary 6 / Theorem 8).

**Q1 second-moment propagation: sound (note).** Each θ appears at one gate, so E[c_A c_B] factorises and vanishes wherever the paths differ in type (1/cos/sin). The derivative maps cos→−sin, sin→cos at (k,q) only: paths differing there still differ, paths agreeing there still agree, so the cancellation survives; E[(f')²] = ½ = E[f²], hence the ½-weight mark. Channels: a Z→I translation branch has type 1 at the next rotation on that qubit while the Z branch (Y after sx) has cos/sin, so the incoherent rule is exact except for the documented CZ-separated pair. Prefix: every post-prefix non-identity qubit is Y-type and is rotated immediately, so distinct post-prefix strings differ in type and squaring after the coefficient-level prefix is exact; c0 = E[C] exactly because every path leaving the prefix carries a cos or sin. Independent check, 2 qubits, L = 2, folded readout (a = 0.97/0.95, b = 0.012/0.02), reset p = 0.25, 0.5 and amplitude damping γ = 0.3, exact 8-point quadrature vs a hand-written chain: E[C], Var[C], Var[∂_{k=1}], Var[∂_{k=2}] agree to all seven printed digits in all three cases (e.g. p = 0.25: Var[∂_{k=2}] 3.886992e-2 both). Also E[C] at n = 53 recomputed from the snapshot: 6.5801e-2, as in PAULIPROP.md.

**Q2 noise model at 10 %, L = 8: fix (should-fix).** (i) Static ZZ acts during the whole 0.35 µs Paper 1 layer, not only the 400 ns dial idle: 0.06 rad per neighbour pair per layer at the 27 kHz median, so the main-grid unital model is missing an effect of the same order as the one now being added for the dial. Add per-edge ZZ for the whole layer to both models. (ii) The incoherent ZZ rule (weight sin²φ to Y_aZ_b, cos²φ stays) is legitimate in this chain: the two branches differ on qubit a (X vs Y → after sx X vs Z, types cos/sin vs 1) at the next rotation, so the missed cross term needs a translation on a first, O(p sin²φ) relative to the ZZ effect; state that residual and validate on a 2×4 density matrix as for the dial. (iii) Readout folding assumes uncorrelated confusion (a_i a_j); Paper 2 Q1 (d) should log the two-qubit confusion on the observable edges.

**Q3 frozen clause (b): fix (should-fix).** Defensible because every amendment preceded any datum and concerned power, not direction, and the record is kept. Two holes: "rungs with the L = 8 reference below 3 shot floors are not counted" must be decided by the *measured* floor-subtracted reference, not the prediction (otherwise it reads as dropping the rung predicted to fail), and "2 of 3" with two counted rungs is "2 of 2": add "clause (b) is inconclusive, not passed, if fewer than two rungs are counted". Cheapest repair of the power problem: run the n = 53 L = 8 p = 0 reference at 16384 shots (floor 3.05e-5, reference 9.2 floors, fall 9 floors; +~3 min at 1 µs), so all three rungs count.

**Q4 light cone with broken couplers: fix (blocker for the n = 39 rung).** From `ladder_placements.json`: on 4×10 the broken coupler (95,96) is adjacent to observable qubit 95, inside every cone from L = 2; on 6×10, 8×10, 10×10 the broken edges (86,87) and (95,96) sit at graph distance 1 from the edge, inside the L ≥ 3 cones. So Section 2's "exactly n-independent at L = 2" holds only for L = 1; at L ≥ 2 the correct statement is cone-graph independence, and n-independence is testable only between rungs with isomorphic cones (53/70, and 87 at L ≤ 4). A same-geometry pre-drawn curve is not a tautology (it is a nontrivial simulation) but it tests the noise model on that graph, not n-dependence; say so. Separately, an "absent" coupler still has its static ZZ (often large on excluded couplers), unmodelled and sitting on the observable qubit at n = 39, a counted Gate 1b rung: move the 4×10 observable edge so no broken coupler lies within distance 2, or model that ZZ.

**Q5 L = 12: fix (should-fix), numbers.** With shot noise dominating, σ(V̂) ≈ F√(2/M): F = 1.22e-4, M = 350 gives 2σ = 1.8e-5, above every L = 12 prediction (4.4e-6 to 6.6e-5 except n = 39 noiseless). Resolving the smallest (4.4e-6) at 2σ needs N ≥ 34k → 65536 shots, 16× cost: reject. Principled alternative at zero shot cost: the same Z-basis shots give ⟨Z_i⟩, ⟨Z_j⟩; register Var[∂⟨Z_i⟩] as the L = 12 secondary observable if its propagated prediction (one-line change of `obs` in `_prefix_coefficients`) exceeds 3 floors = 3.7e-4; otherwise drop L = 12 to the exploratory branch as H6 already allows. Criterion (d)'s fixed 10× allowance (1.22e-3) sits above every L = 8 prediction and can certify nothing; replace by "signal > measured null-control floor + 3 bootstrap σ".

**Q6 dial realisation and what H5–H7 test: sound with additions (should-fix).** E_mask[C_mix] = Tr[O Φ_mix ρ] by linearity (independent Bernoulli masks per qubit and layer give the product channel); Var_θ[C_mix] = Var_θ[C_Φmix] + E_θ Var_mask[C]/K, so subtracting Var_mask/K (gradient: /(2K)) is right, and the exact check confirms 1/K. Condition to write down: the two shift circuits must draw independent masks; with shared masks the last-layer lottery largely cancels in the difference and Var_mask/(2K) over-subtracts. On the floor: (‖t‖²/3)^{|P|} carries the Haar constant 1/3; for uniform Ry the analogue is provable in three lines. In Heisenberg order O → N_p† gives Z_i coefficient c_i = a_i(1−p)(a_j p + b_j); cross terms between the ZZ, Z_i, Z_j pieces vanish under the last layer's angles; E_θ⟨Z_i⟩² = (z_i²+x_i²)/2 ≥ z_i²/2 with z_i = (1−p)z_i^{aCZ} + p and E[z_i^{aCZ}] = 0, so E z_i² ≥ p². Hence, rigorously for this ansatz and channel, Var[∂_{k=L}C] ≥ ½ c_i² g_i² p² and Var[C] ≥ that plus the j term (g = last-layer depolarising factor). Snapshot numbers at n = 53: k = L ≥ 1.06e-3 (p = 0.25), 7.35e-3 (0.5); Var[C] ≥ 2.20e-3, 1.50e-2 — 5× and 2.2× above p⁴/9, L- and n-independent, and the predictions clear them by 1.88/1.30 (k = L) and 1.62/1.22 (C). Draw this line, not p⁴/9, as the refutation threshold; the k = 1 series has no such floor, which is the contrast.

**Q7 Paper 2: fix (blocker for H3 as written).** Q2: the 25 ns / 0.9375 coefficient presumes the reset's emptying time, which is what Paper 2 measures; fit τ_eff from the |1⟩-arm excess vs ζ across pairs (slope = τ_eff, a new deliverable) and test the residual against 5 mrad. Q3: the frame twirl converts static ZZ into a Pauli channel but does not remove it: per cycle per neighbour ≈ (ζ·400 ns/2)² ≈ 1.2e-3 at 27 kHz, and dense and sparse masks differ in neighbour composition, so a known unital mechanism alone can breach the 1e-3 bound. Predict and subtract it from the Q2 ζ map, or add a mid-cycle spectator echo, before the bound means crosstalk.

**Q8 Deviation 14 ratio: fix (should-fix).** At native noise the predicted R_nonunital/R_unital is 0.96–1.10 with 2σ 0.06–0.5: no power; say so in Section 3. Under the dial the k = 1 denominator (3.5e-6 to 1.5e-8) is below the shot floor, so the ratio is undefined. The clean separator at this cost is the k = L depth ratio V(12)/V(8) (≈1 dial vs 0.02–0.04 unital) plus the absolute k = L separation, which Gate 1b already uses; report k = 1 as upper bounds.

**Q9 kurtosis: note.** A single path's coefficient has kurtosis (3/2)^{#branchings}; sums of paths lighten this, so κ grows with depth and falls with noise (depolarising reweights toward short paths) and with n (more paths in a cone-covered patch), so heavier tails at n = 39/87 are unlikely but unmeasured. L = 12 κ is irrelevant: σ_12 < 4e-5 even at κ = 100. Log-variance and trimmed estimators change the estimand: reject. Keep M = 350 and add a pre-registered on-day rule: if the sample κ at the L = 8 reference exceeds 21, top up to M = 600 from reserve (covers κ ≤ 35) rather than amend.

**Q10 first attack: should-fix.** "Your k = L variance is dominated by the last-layer term ½c_i²p² that a reset trivially creates; a 2-qubit circuit shows it, and everything is classically predicted." Pre-empt by making the ratio measured k = L variance / rigorous floor (predicted 1.88 at p = 0.25, 1.30 at 0.5) the headline quantity — it is the model-dependent, deeper-path content — and by phrasing Section 3b as a variance-level benchmark of a first-principles noise model, not a demonstration of simulability or of Theorem 10 (which assumes 2-design layers; Mele et al. themselves note the extension beyond that is numerical).
