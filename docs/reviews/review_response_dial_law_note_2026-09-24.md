25 Sep 2026 · session f3ae754f · referee frame d3b1fd24 · verdict GO_WITH_CORRECTIONS (3 must-fix, 8 should-fix, 13 optional)

The referee confirmed the derivation (Lemma 1, the truncation identities, the same-mask rule, closed forms A–C and their lower-bound property). It reproduced the chain with its own codes: an exact two-copy propagation on 2×3 (28 quantities, ≤ 2.1e-12) and a Monte Carlo on 3×3 (15 quantities within 1.8 s.e.). It also confirmed the repository claims against the packet and the four references. Its declared limits: the Monte Carlo draw counts were reduced (3.6–14 % precision); the repository was checked from the packet only; the 52-qubit chain was not rerun; the ℓ = 4 resolvability estimate is provisional. All items were applied to the note (version 3) as follows.

## Must-fix

- **M1 (ℓ = 4 is near the detection limit, not an upper bound by statistics).** Accepted. The summary, Table 3 text, Section 4 (H7) and Section 6 now say that ℓ = 4 lies below one draw's shot s.d. but, over 100 draws with the shot term subtracted, sits near the detection limit (z ≈ 2.2–2.6, referee's provisional estimate). Section 6 item 3 asks the Deviation to choose one rule, (a) upper bound by rule or (b) statistical flag, with its expected outcome, and not to combine them.
- **M2 (guard, and how the missing producer was established).** Accepted. Section 6 item 1 adds the guard: H7 is not-evaluable without `rms_l2`. The absence of a producer is now established directly: a grep of all 69 Python files and 21 prediction JSONs at main 36e5f2b (25 Sep) finds `rms_l2` only in `evaluate_h7` and no `truncation` key in any prediction file. The `_value_test` behaviour for a missing sigma was read from the code: it uses the measured interval only.
- **M3 (pre-specified comparator procedure).** Accepted. The 10 % "investigate" guidance and the H7-only model-error term are removed. The procedure is now: an ideal-model bug check of the engine against this note's chain, where a failure triggers a documented code audit, never a choice of value; the snapshot-noise value committed as produced; `rms_l2_sigma` equal to the engine's truncation or sampling error, following the H5/H6 convention; and an explicit statement of √MSD versus √(MSD + shot²).

## Should-fix

- **S1 (second transient as ℓ → L).** Accepted; Section 2(C) and the summary now say that √λ₁ ≈ √r applies when both ℓ and L − ℓ are large, give both transients, and note that at the H7 point the effective factor is within 1.1 % of √r.
- **S2 (Table 4 and Figure b asymptote).** Accepted. Both now use √λ₁ from the lone-Z weight ratio at L = 16 (ℓ = 7 → 8): 0.3540, 0.5321 and 0.6067 at p = 0.5, 0.25 and 0.15.
- **S3 (δ-convergence sentence).** Accepted in part. The sentence now separates Var, V_L and V_1 and MSD(ℓ ≤ 4) (≤ 1.3e-3) from MSD(5–7) at p = 0.5 (up to 1.1e-2), and adds the caution that any comparator must use δ well below the MSD scale. Not accepted: "the truncated MSD is not a lower bound". Pruning before the cut removes a string together with all its descendants, whose total contribution Σ w Δ is non-negative. Pruning after the cut lowers E[C²] only. So the truncated MSD is at most the exact MSD in exact arithmetic. A slightly negative value near the truncation scale (−1.6e-10 at p = 0.7, ℓ = 7) is a lower bound that has fallen below zero, not a violation. The note now says this explicitly.
- **S4 ("the law is the value", "no free parameter").** Accepted. The chain gives the value in the ideal model and the law is its leading-order closed form. "No free parameter" now reads "no fitted parameter (ideal model); noise enters to first order through calibrated f, g, t_z, D_z".
- **S5 (free exponent).** Accepted. Both numbers are given, 1.05 for the true RMS and 0.84 for the statistic as coded, as interpretation only.
- **S6 (Deviation 33 wording).** Accepted. The floor is the first part of the j = 1 term; the rest, p⁴(1 − p)⁴/4, is stated.
- **S7 (uncertainty of the RMS).** Accepted. Table 3's text gives the referee's provisional half-widths (15–28 % on RMS(2), about 40 % on RMS(4)), so that the 1 % shot bias is not mistaken for the test's resolution.
- **S8 (selection and pairing of the truncation rows).** Upgraded to must-fix after verification at main 36e5f2b. `_point_id` in `gradvar/analysis/loader.py` (lines 126–133) gives `trunc_full_p0.5_L8`, `trunc_l2_p0.5_L8` and the H5/H6 point `dial_p0.5_L8_kL` the same id, `reset p0.5 n52 L8 k8 r0`, and `evaluate_h5` / `evaluate_h6` select by kind, arm and k = L. The truncation rows would be pooled into the p = 0.5, L = 8 dial point, and the repeat index would interleave the full and truncated rows (shared seed 23291001). This is Section 6 item 2.
