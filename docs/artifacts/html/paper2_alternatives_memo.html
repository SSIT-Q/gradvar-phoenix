Verdict. Build Paper 2 around the reset element as a calibrated non-unitality dial (expected rank 1). Keep the classical-surrogate stress test (Nature-upside rank 1) as a gated arm; promote it only if a tensor-network partner signs by mid-November. The rest are Paper 1 figures or the bin.

Recommended paper, stand-alone: 20% PRX Quantum or Quantum, 60% QST or npj QI, 85% as a Paper 1 section, 2% Nature family.

One number governs every row. A standard point is 100 draws × 2 shift circuits × 4096 shots = 819,200 executions. IBM's run-time formula is 2 s + (rep_delay + circuit length) × executions. At the default rep_delay of 250 µs that is 3.5 to 3.7 min per point (4.8 min by IBM's 0.35 ms shortcut, kept as the upper figure in the table). At the ~1 µs idle the Nighthawk r2 release advertises it is 0.12 min with a fast dissipative reset (~5 µs circuit) to 0.33 min with measure-based reset (~21 µs), so 2 to 5 min for direction 1's 15-point grid. A factor 15 to 30 separates the two regimes. The 1 µs figure presupposes the dissipative reset element works at every shot start, since T1 = 178 µs rules out passive relaxation in 1 µs: the budget and direction 1 share that one risk. backend.rep_delay_range may not permit 1 µs at all. Nothing is booked until the dry run reads default_rep_delay and rep_delay_range, measures locked time per point, and confirms Paper 1's own budget, which an earlier review found already tight in 180 min at default rep_delay; direction 1 fits in the remaining minutes only after that confirmation.

EXPECTED 1NATURE 2

The non-unitality dial

Hypothesis. Realise the i.i.d. mixture channel Np(ρ) = (1−p)ρ + p|0⟩⟨0| on every qubit after every layer of a 6×10 Ry/CZ circuit: a fresh random reset mask is drawn per layer and per circuit, the observed pair is in the lottery, and K = 64 masks are pooled per parameter draw (64 shots each, same 4096-shot execution count), so the estimator targets Cmix(θ), the pattern-averaged cost. For this channel t = (0,0,p), D = (1−p)I, c = (1−p)² + p²/3. Two separate hypotheses, p ∈ {0.1, 0.25, 0.5}, p = 0 being Paper 1's grid. H-A (Mele Cor. 6): Varθ[Cmix] is unchanged between L = 8 and 12 and ≥ (1/3)p⁴ (3×10⁻⁵, 1.3×10⁻³, 2.1×10⁻²). H-B (Mele Thm 8): Var[∂kCmix] ∝ exp(−α(p)(L−k)) with α rising in p and the k = L point L-independent; at p = 0 every k decays with L. Pooling adds a pattern-noise floor: Varmask[C]/K on Cmix beside the shot floor 1/4096, and Varmask[C]/(2K) on the gradient beside 1/(2·4096), both drawn on every plot. Varmask[C] is estimated per draw from the spread of the 64 per-mask 64-shot means minus their shot variance.

Why this form
With one mask per draw the per-qubit channel is identity or replacer, second moments do not factorise (E[N⊗N] ≠ Np⊗Np), the floor is nearer (1/3)p² and the theorems do not apply; the killed memo's dissrand ansatz was that configuration and showed a flat k-profile. Per-shot randomness is unavailable in a static circuit, so pooled per-circuit masks are the practical approximation.
Theory
Mele et al. 2403.13927 Thm 1 (effective log depth), Thm 5/Cor. 6, Thm 8 (2-design layers, average case); Singkanipa–Lidar 2402.08721; Angrisani et al. 2501.13101 cover constant-rate dynamic non-unitary operations, so the baseline is cheap.
Truncation arm
Per draw at p ∈ {0.25, 0.5}, also run the circuit with its first L−ℓ layers deleted and all qubits started in |0⟩, ℓ ∈ {2, 4}, one circuit per draw at 16384 shots, and report the RMS over the 100 draws of Cmix − Cmix[L−ℓ,L] against ℓ and p. This is Theorem 1's "noise-induced shallow circuit" statement measured at 60 qubits as an observable difference rather than a gradient variance: the RMS difference falls roughly as c(p)ℓ/2·stdθ(Cmix). Resolvability sets the shot count: stdθ(Cmix) is at least 0.036 (p = 0.25) and 0.14 (p = 0.5) from the Cor. 6 floor, against a shot std of 0.016 at 4096 shots and 0.008 at 16384, so at 4096 shots only p = 0.5, ℓ = 2 is resolved, while 16384 resolves both p at ℓ = 2 and p = 0.5 at ℓ = 4; p = 0.1 (std ≥ 0.0055) is invisible and is not run. There is no clean p = 0 control at this depth: by Paper 1's own hypothesis the p = 0 cost at n = 60, L = 8 is in a plateau and both circuits sit at the shot floor. With the resolved shot budget this is the figure that lifts a QST confirmation to PRX Quantum, because it is the headline claim of a Nature Physics theory paper shown on hardware with the non-unitality dialled.
Why hardware
Native T1 cannot test any of this: one layer is 0.35 µs against T1 = 178 µs, γ ≈ 2×10⁻³, and the Cor. 6 floor (1/3)γ⁴ ≈ 5×10⁻¹² is what Paper 1's H3 tests; p = 0.1 is the point that rescues H3. Schmitt et al. 2602.22851 blame T1 for their saturation with no knob to turn; the 120 reset elements are that knob, unique to this device, with real Pauli, readout and reset error on top.
Cost
Gradient grid at n = 60: 3 p × L = 8 × k ∈ {L, L−1, L−2, L−4} (12 points) plus k = L at L = 12 for each p (3 points, needed for the L-independence claims in H-A and H-B; Var[Cmix] at L = 8 comes free from the shift circuits): 15 points, 53–55 min at 250 µs (72 by the shortcut), 2–5 min at 1 µs. Truncation arm: 2 p × 2 ℓ × 100 draws = 400 circuits at 16384 shots, ~28 min at 250 µs, 0.7–2.4 min at 1 µs. Reset-error characterisation ~3 min, rep_delay-independent (duration is read from backend.target["reset"][(q,)].duration; only the error needs measuring). Total 84–86 min at 250 µs (103 by the shortcut), 6–10 min at 1 µs. Extension if minutes allow: n = 100 at p = 0.5, 4 points, +15 min.
Baseline
Pauli propagation with the mixture rule (Z→(1−p)Z, X,Y→(1−p)X,Y, plus p·I from the trace term); exact to 12 qubits (memo code, extended from the reset rule). Nearest: 2403.13927, 2402.08721, 2602.22851, 2507.02043. No fetched paper has done this; "uncontested" is unconfirmed by an arXiv search, not established.
Main risk
Mid-circuit reset compiled as measure + conditional X (~2 µs per layer, uncontrolled γ ≈ 10⁻²). Kill if reset error exceeds 2×10⁻² or the pattern-noise floor at K = 64 exceeds the predicted p = 0.1 separation, unless simulation with measured values still separates the p levels by twice the combined floor.
Odds
20 / 60 / 85
Nature-worthy
α(p) or the floor at 100 qubits departs from the average-case prediction and noisy simulation cannot reproduce it: an engineered non-unital circuit trainable beyond Mele's effective depth. P ≈ 2%. Lift: an NQM-hub theorist (IISc) for the non-2-design prediction; variable-strength reset from IBM; +100 min for a full (n, L, p) grid.
Theorist's additions (19 Sep 2026)
From the theorist's second opinion. (1) Add a qubit-number ladder: n ∈ {40, 60, 100} at p = 0.25, L = 8, k = L. Corollaries 6 and 9 are statements about n and the grid above has no n axis; drop the k = L−4 points to pay for it. (2) Frame H-A and H-B as a second-moment prediction for this Ry/CZ ansatz, drawn before data, not as a test of Theorem 8: the ansatz is not a local 2-design (Ry leaves Y-strings unrotated, so Y-paths form a separate slow mode), so the theorem supplies the form, not the constants. (3) The truncation arm is the headline figure: Theorem 1 measured as an observable difference at 60–100 qubits with no ensemble variance. (4) Subtract the pattern-noise term Varmask[C]/K (and /(2K) on the gradient) like shot noise, rather than quoting it as a floor. (5) Report p = 0.1 as exploratory; its floors sit at the shot floor. (6) Run a rotation-survival check for measure + X reset at p > 0: the reset noise is θ-independent only if the measurement is.

Referee: "You mixed a reset channel into a circuit and confirmed the circuit forgets its early layers; a density-matrix simulation gives the same curve to three digits."

EXPECTED 2NATURE 1

Classical-surrogate stress test

Hypothesis. Sweep angle width σ (θ ~ N(0,σ²) about identity; σ ∈ {0.1, 0.3, 0.6, 1.0} plus uniform) on 6×10 and 10×10 at L = 8, and L = 12 at n = 100. Per-draw correlation between hardware gradients and truncated Pauli propagation (weights 4–8, δ-sweep after 2507.10771) and MPS (χ ≤ 512) exceeds 0.95 at small σ, is undefined at uniform (all at the floor), and dips in a σ window where Var[∂C] is still ≥ 10× the floor; device noise narrows the window (2501.13101) but does not close it at L = 8. Absorbs brief candidates 2 and 3.

Theory
2409.01706, 2501.13101 (locally scrambling and noisy circuits simulable on average); 2411.19896 (patch surrogates); 2312.09121 (the dichotomy); 2508.06358 (low-weight propagation an unreliable estimator).
Why hardware
Inside the window no classical method is converged, so hardware is the only reference, with real rather than assumed noise. [Struck 19 Sep 2026 after theorist review: a truncation residual does not imply hardness; average-case simulability is proven (Angrisani et al. 2501.13101).] Clifford-anchored controls (some angles at multiples of π/2, exact value known) calibrate hardware accuracy at the same depth, after 2603.14485.
Cost
5 σ × 2 n + 3 at L = 12 + 4 anchors = 17 points, 82 min at 250 µs, ~4 min at 1 µs.
Baseline
Heavy: PauliPropagation.jl (2505.21606) with Paper 1's learned Pauli–Lindblad model; MPS on 6×10 only; PEPS at depth 8 is out of reach on four cores. Nearest: 2603.14485 (49 q, no gradients), 2602.04676 (classical half), 2607.17804 (classical gradients, 100 q).
Main risk
Without anchors a discrepancy is indistinguishable from noise-model error; 2308.05077 shows classical methods catching up within a year.
Odds
25 / 55 / 70 with a tensor-network partner; 10–15 / 45 / 70 without
Nature-worthy
At 100 qubits, L ≥ 8: an anchored σ window with gradients ≥ 10× the floor where Pauli propagation and MPS both fail to converge. [Struck 19 Sep 2026 after theorist review: a truncation residual does not imply hardness; average-case simulability is proven (Angrisani et al. 2501.13101).] P ≈ 3%, lower without a classical partner. Lift: a tensor-network group (IISc, TIFR, ICTS) for PEPS and belief-propagation baselines; a 156-qubit Heron cross-check; +150 min for 16384-shot per-draw correlations.

Referee: "Angrisani et al. prove the average case simulable, so your window is a truncation artefact."

Mitigation inflation as the object

Hypothesis. At fixed total shots, ZNE via probabilistic error amplification (gains 1, 1.3, 1.6) inflates gradient-estimator variance by a factor growing exponentially in n·L while draw-to-draw signal variance is unchanged: mitigation moves the mean, not the resolvability. TREX inflates by the readout factor only.

Theory
Wang et al. 2109.01051; Quek et al. 2210.11505; Tsubouchi et al. 2208.09385; van den Berg et al. 2201.09866 (γ²); 2012.09738 (TREX).
Why hardware
γ for a 2D 100-qubit sparse Pauli–Lindblad layer is unreported; 2604.19426 saw 3–5× inflation at p = 1 QAOA on ibm_fez with no depth or size scan.
Cost
{40, 100} × {L = 4, 8} × (TREX + 3 gains) = 16 sets, 77 min at 250 µs, ~3 min at 1 µs, plus 5–10 min noise learning; fractional gates off.
Main risk

Recommendation

Primary: the non-unitality dial. Only this device can run it; native T1 leaves the non-unital regime twelve orders of magnitude below the shot floor. It pre-registers two functional forms (H-A floor, H-B layer-index decay) and a direct Theorem 1 truncation test rather than a search, its baseline is deliverable on four cores by January, and its p = 0 arm is Paper 1's grid while p = 0.1 rescues Paper 1's H3, so if Paper 1 runs short the dial folds in as its non-unital arm and the merged paper is stronger than either. Budget 84–86 min at default rep_delay at n = 60 (53–55 gradient grid, 28 truncation arm, 3 characterisation), after Paper 1's budget is confirmed; at ~1 µs, add n = 100 at all p and the p = 1 endpoint.

The trade-off. The rankings disagree at the top. The surrogate test has the higher Nature ceiling (3% against 2%): a measurable gradient where every classical method fails is a claim Nature Physics has published before. [Struck 19 Sep 2026 after theorist review: a truncation residual does not imply hardness; average-case simulability is proven (Angrisani et al. 2501.13101).] Reaching it needs a tensor-network partner and CPU-weeks the team lacks; without them its PRX Quantum odds are 10–15%, below the dial's 20%. The dial's ceiling is lower because a deviation from an average-case theorem invites "your ansatz is not a 2-design", which only a theorist co-author turns into a result. Nothing here exceeds 3%.

Decision rule. Pre-register the dial now. If by 15 November an NQM-hub tensor-network group has agreed to co-author and PauliPropagation.jl δ-sweeps on the 10×10 patch at L = 8 fail to converge for some σ with predicted Var[∂C] ≥ 10× the floor, swap: surrogate test primary (80 min), dial reduced to p ∈ {0.1, 0.5} at L = 8 plus the two L = 12 points and the p = 0.5 truncation arm (~50 min). [Struck 19 Sep 2026 after theorist review: a truncation residual does not imply hardness; average-case simulability is proven (Angrisani et al. 2501.13101).] Otherwise the dial runs in full and the last 30–40 min go to the σ window with anchors or to Paper 1 repeats. Ranks 3–6 are not pre-registered as papers.
