Ten questions we most want challenged

  1. Is the second-moment Pauli-propagation derivation correct?

    Cross terms are said to vanish under uniform θ because distinct paths differ in factor type (1, cos, sin) at some rotation; the derivative is a ½-weight mark at gate (k, q). Does the cancellation survive the derivative and the channels?

  2. Is the noise model adequate for a 10% prediction at L = 8?

    Depolarising from per-edge CZ and 1Q errors, T1/T2 per CZ sub-layer, readout confusion, the dial Np = p·Reset + (1−p)·Idle(400 ns), and the per-edge ZZ idle phase now being added (worth 10–20% on the p = 0 reference). What is missing at that level?

  3. Is the Gate 1b clause (b) history defensible?

    The unital-reference clause was rewritten three times before any datum (Deviations 28→30), each time because prediction showed the previous wording unresolvable. Is the frozen criterion, the L = 8→12 fall of the delay-matched p = 0 series judged per rung with 2 of 3 passing, the right one?

  4. Does the light-cone argument survive broken couplers?

    Couplers with CZ error ≥ 5e-3 are absent edges; the 39- and 87-qubit patches carry 5 and 7, so the predicted p = 0 series is non-monotone in n. Does n-independence at L ≤ 4 hold, and is a curve pre-drawn on the same broken geometry a test or a tautology?

  5. Depth 12 and the criterion (d) allowance.

    Every predicted L = 12 variance lies below the 4096-shot floor, and the 10× hardware-floor allowance sits above every L = 8 prediction. More shots, drop L = 12, or a different observable?

  6. Does the reset dial realise the i.i.d. non-unital channel?

    A fresh mask per layer and circuit, pooled over K = 256 masks, gives Np in the mean; the lottery's variance is subtracted as a pattern floor Varmask[C]/(2K). Is that right, and since Ry/CZ is not a local 2-design, what exactly do H5–H7 test?

  7. Is Paper 2's backaction and ZZ design under-specified?

    Q2: target-|0⟩ primary arm, ζ-corrected |1⟩ arm with the 375/400 coefficient. Q3: frame-tracked reset cycle benchmark. Is the ZZ model right when a working reset empties the target in about 25 ns, and does the twirl hold for a reset that erases the frame?

  8. Is the layer-index ratio the discriminating statistic?

    Is the noiseless-normalised ratio Var(k = L)/Var(k = 1) (Deviation 14) the cleanest separator of unital from non-unital noise at this shot cost?

  9. Heavy tails and draw counts.

    The k = L gradient kurtosis measured 14.2 against the 8.4 assumed; M = 350 keeps the 2× margin to κ ≈ 21. Any reason to expect heavier tails under noise or at larger n?

  10. What would a PRX Quantum referee attack first?

    If the data came out exactly as predicted: the simulability conclusion of Section 3b, the gap to the 2-design theorems, the pre-data amendments, or something we have not seen?
