\section{Purpose and scope}
\begin{enumerate}
\item State, for the specific ansatz of Paper 1 (one $R_y$ layer on every qubit of a rectangular square-lattice patch, four CZ sub-layers on the patch edges, $L$ layers, uniform angles), the exact second-moment map that gives $\Var_\theta[\partial_{(k,q)}\langle Z_iZ_j\rangle]$ and $\Var_\theta[\langle Z_iZ_j\rangle]$ under (i) no noise, (ii) local unital noise, (iii) local non-unital noise, (iv) the reset dial $\Np = p\,\mathrm{Reset} + (1-p)\,\mathrm{Idle}(400\,\mathrm{ns})$.
\item Relate the functional forms measured in Paper 1 (H5--H7) to the ensemble theorems of Mele et al.\ (Nat.\ Phys.\ 22, 751; Corollary 6, Theorem 8, Proposition 10), Singkanipa and Lidar (Quantum 9, 1617) and the Pauli-propagation guarantees of Angrisani et al.\ (PRX Quantum 7, 020313), making precise what is and is not tested when the ansatz is not a local 2-design.
\item Supply the two models Paper 2 needs from a theorist: the $ZZ$ phase during a reset that empties the target in $\sim$25\,ns (Q2), and the frame-tracked reset cycle benchmark for a reset without a classical register (Q3).
\end{enumerate}

\section{The second-moment calculation (to be done)}
\subsection{Setting and notation}
Heisenberg back-propagation of the readout-folded $O = Z_iZ_j$ through the transpiled circuit; Pauli-path expansion $\langle O\rangle(\theta) = \sum_{\text{paths}} c_{\text{path}}(\theta)$, $c_{\text{path}} = \text{const}\times\prod_g f_g(\theta_g)$ with $f_g \in \{1, \cos, \sin\}$. Definitions of the path set, branchings, the $\theta$-independent prefix.

\subsection{Lemma 1: vanishing of cross terms under uniform angles}
\textbf{To prove.} For $\theta$ uniform on $[0, 2\pi)^{nL}$, two distinct paths have zero covariance; the argument in \texttt{docs/PAULIPROP.md} (distinct paths differ in factor type at some rotation; given identical types the Cliffords, branch choices and noise branchings followed by a rotation on the same qubit reproduce the same path) is to be written as a proof, including the case of noise branchings not followed by a rotation (the prefix) and the two $Z \to I$ relaxation branches separated by a CZ that the code treats as distinct (recorded gap, relative $< 10^{-3}$). \emph{Referee brief Q1.}

\subsection{Lemma 2: the derivative as a $\tfrac12$-weight mark}
\textbf{To prove.} $\partial_{(k,q)}$ keeps exactly the paths whose Pauli does not commute with $Y_q$ at gate $(k, q)$, with the same $\tfrac12$ weight ($\partial\cos = -\sin$, $\partial\sin = \cos$), and $\mathbb{E}[\partial\langle O\rangle] = 0$. Show the cancellation of Lemma 1 survives differentiation and the channel maps.

\subsection{Proposition 3: the second moment as a positive linear map}
\textbf{To state and prove.} Rotation about axis $A$: $P \notin \{I, A\} \mapsto \tfrac12$ on each of the two other non-identity Paulis; Clifford: permutation; single-qubit channel $r \mapsto Dr + t$ (diagonal $D$): $P_a \mapsto D_a^2 P_a$, $t_a^2 \mapsto I$; Pauli channel: $(1-\lambda)^2$ on non-identity strings. Total weight non-increasing under every op, hence the truncation lower bound $V_{\mathrm{trunc}} \leq V$ (Proposition 4).

\subsection{Proposition 4: rigorous lower bound and error statement}
Deterministic truncation by coefficient is a lower bound; the discarded mass is not an error bound (85\% of the mass carries 3--5\% of the variance at $L \geq 8$); the unbiased Pauli-path sampler gives $V_{\mathrm{MC}} \pm 2\sigma$; the Deviation 15 error is $\max(2\sigma, V_{\mathrm{MC}} - V_{\mathrm{trunc}})$. \tbd{Can a two-sided bound be obtained from the weight of the truncated strings times a bound on their final factors?}

\subsection{The dial channel}
Bloch form of $\Np$ on top of the snapshot unital noise: $D = (1-p)(e^{-400/T_2}, e^{-400/T_2}, 1)$, $t_z = p$; contraction $c = (\|D\|_F^2 + \|t\|^2)/3$; delay-matched control $p = 0$; dephasing dial $t = 0$. \textbf{To do:} (a) the pattern-noise floor $\mathbb{E}_\theta\Var_{\mathrm{mask}}[C]$ as a second propagation with a deterministic reset per path, and why the pooled estimator's excess variance is $\Var_{\mathrm{mask}}[C]/(2K)$ (checked numerically at $K = 64, 256$: ratio $3.50 \pm 0.84$ against 4); (b) the mask-dependent $ZZ$ idle phase as a two-qubit unital rotation of angle $J\tau \sim 10^{-2}$\,rad per pair per layer, and idle-branch $T_1$ ($t_z \sim 2\times10^{-3}$), both currently absent from the model and worth 10--20\% on the $p = 0$, $L = 8$ reference (Deviation 30 correction; pre-Gate-2 action); (c) $\mathbb{E}_\theta[C_{\mathrm{mix}}] = p^2$ before readout folding as an exact identity of the last reset layer. \emph{Referee brief Q2, Q6.}

\section{Theorems to prove or cite}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cite.} Mele et al.: Corollary 6 (cost variance $\Theta(1)$ with floor $(\|t\|_2^2/3)^{|P|}$), Theorem 8 ($\Var[\partial_k C] = e^{-\Theta(L-k)}$), Proposition 10 (unital upper bound $e^{-\Omega(|P|+L)}$), Theorem 1 (noise-induced effective depth), Proposition 3 / Theorem 4 (average-case simulability); all on average over local 2-design gate layers. State explicitly that Ry/CZ is not a local 2-design (Uvarov--Biamonte Table 1, $\lambda_1 = 1.76$) so no constant is tested, only functional forms against the ansatz-specific curve.
\item \textbf{Cite.} Singkanipa--Lidar: HS-contractive channels, the noisy parameter-shift rule; check that $\Np$ and the delay-matched idle channel are in the class and that the shift rule remains exact for the pooled estimator.
\item \textbf{Cite.} Angrisani et al.: average-case Pauli-propagation simulability under arbitrary local noise; state the ensemble and precision the guarantee covers and that at Nighthawk noise levels and gradient-scale precision the runtime bound is not informative, so the classical baseline is demonstrated numerically (verification report, cross-cutting correction 7).
\item \textbf{Prove (ansatz-specific).} Exact $n$-independence of the noiseless second moment while the patch contains the cone; its failure mode with broken couplers (the 39- and 87-qubit patches carry 5 and 7); whether a curve pre-drawn on the same broken geometry is a test or a tautology. \emph{Referee brief Q4.}
\item \textbf{Prove or bound.} The $k = L$ flatness in $L$ and $n$ under $\Np$ for this ansatz: identify the terms of the second-moment map that survive the last reset layer and give the leading constant in $p$; compare with $p^4/9$.
\item \textbf{Prove.} The truncation-arm statistic: $\mathrm{RMS}_\theta[C_{\mathrm{mix}} - C_{\mathrm{mix}}[L-\ell, L]] \approx c^{\ell/2}\,\mathrm{std}_\theta(C_{\mathrm{mix}})$ up to an ansatz constant; the residual pattern noise of the deleted layers and its subtraction.
\item \textbf{Assess.} Is the noiseless-normalised layer-index ratio (Deviation 14) the cleanest separator of unital from non-unital noise at this shot cost, and what would be? \emph{Referee brief Q8.}
\item \textbf{Assess.} Heavy tails: measured $k = L$ kurtosis 14.2 at $(n = 20, L = 8)$ noiseless; expected behaviour under noise and at larger $n$; implications for the $M = 350$ booking (2$\times$ margin to $\kappa \approx 21$). \emph{Referee brief Q9.}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Paper 2 models}
\subsection{$ZZ$ during reset (Q2)}
Model the spectator phase in the reset arm when the target's population decays on a time scale $\tau_r \ll 400$\,ns: $\phi_{\mathrm{reset}} = \zeta\int_0^{400\,\mathrm{ns}} n_t(t)\,dt$ against $\phi_{\mathrm{delay}} = \zeta\times400$\,ns$\times n_t(0)$; the pre-registered subtraction uses $0.9375 = 375/400$ with $\tau_r \approx 25$\,ns. \tbd{Derive the correction as a function of $\tau_r$; propagate the uncertainty in $\tau_r$ (unmeasured) into the 1.16\,mrad floor; state what the target-$|0\rangle$ arm cancels exactly.} \emph{Referee brief Q7.}
\subsection{Frame-tracked reset cycle benchmark (Q3)}
Show that with a reset in place of the MCM the twirled channel on the reset qubit is the reset itself up to the final frame, that \texttt{expected\_z} is the correct frame bookkeeping, and that the spectator error is twirled to a Pauli channel whose per-cycle decay in $m$ is what the fit extracts; identify the learnable parameters under SPAM in the sense of Zhang et al.\ and the gauge freedom of Chu et al.
