## 5. The day-3 science checks (evaluated at the post-run review; pipeline verdicts H5 to H7, Gate 1b clause (b), dial controls)

- **Gate 1b clause (b), live**: per rung, floor-subtracted Var(k = L, L = 8) minus Var(L = 12) on the measured references against the Deviation 45 bar
  (3 x the larger shot floor, 9.2e-5 at 16384) and 2 x the L = 8 point's bootstrap 2 sigma; counted rungs need the L = 8 reference above 3 shot floors;
  pass = at least two counted rungs all passing (Deviations 30, 35). Read against the re-drawn predictions of Section 2.5 (the 20 Sep readings: fall / 3 floors
  7.8 / 2.8 / 3.9 at n40 / n60 / n100 under the Deviation 34 model).
- **H5** (Deviation 33 floor): floor-subtracted Var[C_mix] at p = 0.25 / 0.5, L = 8 / 12 on n60 against the pre-drawn values and the floor
  1/2 p^2 (c_i^2 g_i^2 + c_j^2 g_j^2) with its interval; the depth ratio Var(L = 12) / Var(L = 8) at each p against its pre-drawn ratio.
- **H6**: k = L variance above the Deviation 33 floor at every dial point; depth ratio at each p; the ladder ratio Var(n100) / Var(n40) at p = 0.25; the reset
  dial at n60 L = 8 above the dephasing dial by the pre-drawn factor; k = 1 rows are upper bounds (Deviation 40; contingent list).
- **H7**: RMS of C_mix - C_mix[L - 2, L] against the pre-drawn prediction (l = 4 contingent).
- Deviation 38 masks: shared between the shift circuits, logged per pub; Deviation 19 flags on the dial points against the re-drawn rows.
