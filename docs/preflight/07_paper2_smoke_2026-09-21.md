## 6. Kill criteria (1)-(3) and Gate P2-2: what the post-run review checks, with the numbers

**Kill criteria (Section 4 of the pre-registration; any one ends the Paper 2 hardware stage, the minutes return to the reserve).**

- **(1) reset compiles to `measure_reset` or any 1940 ns instruction**: in every native-reset circuit `circuits.json["ops"]` carries `reset`
  (118 per Q1 (a) / (b) / (f), 236 / 472 for (c), 22 r on Q2, 22 m / 47 m on Q3, 6 / 4 / 10 on Q4) and **`ops["measure"] == n_measured`**
  (118, or 24 on Q4), `reset_ns_target` = 400.0 on all 118 qubits and `sched_ns` of `Q1b_reset` minus `Q1d_measure` about 400 ns (not 1,940);
  `synthetic_target_instructions` empty. Fires if any native-reset circuit shows extra measures, a 1,940 ns reset or `measure_reset` in its ops.
- **(2) native reset error median above 2e-2**: median over the 118 qubits of P(1 \| `Q1a_reset`) - P(1 \| `Q1d_measure`) (raw P(1) reported
  beside it). Reference: Paper 1's 20-qubit smoke median 2.4e-3 raw. Line **2e-2**.
- **(3) locked time above 1.5x the Deviation 2 estimate**: read **per execution** as Gate P2-2 words it: `metrics().circuits_execution_time_ns`
  / executions against the model's **16.28 us (init_true) and 14.16 us (init_false)** at the 1 us default -> lines **24.4 us and 21.2 us**
  (Paper 1 measured 9.2-14.1 us per execution on SPAM-only and shallow circuits; the Q3 m = 64 circuits at about 33 us each are 8 percent of
  the executions and are in the model). Logged beside it, not decisive: the whole-job ratio `qpu_charge_time_seconds` / 3.23 s and / 2.09 s,
  expected 1.4-2.1 from the 3-5 s job floors alone (Section 2.1), and the list total against 5.32 s (line 7.98 s under a literal reading). If
  the per-execution ratio also exceeds **1.27x** (the campaign's tolerance to the 45-minute cap, Deviation 2), Q2 reverts to 8,192 shots as
  pre-registered.

**Gate P2-2 (after the smoke test, before Q1).**

- **(i) reset acted, delay did not**: on at least **90 percent of the 118 qubits (107)** P(1 \| `Q1a_reset`) lies within 3 sigma of P(1 \|
  `Q1d_measure`) (sigma from the two 2,048-shot binomials, about 4.6e-3 at the median p(1\|0) of 2.4e-3, larger on the flagged qubits) **and**
  P(1 \| `Q1e_x_delay_measure`) exceeds **0.9** (expected about 0.98 from p(0\|1) median 1.2e-2). Reported for all three reset kinds, decided on
  the native one; the measurement-based kinds are reported with their `mcm` marginals (expected about 1 on the (a) arms, about 0 on (b)).
- **(ii) durations and instructions**: `reset_ns_target` **400 ns** on all 118 qubits (2,140 ns on qubit 79 is checked in the Q1 list, not
  here); the three reset kinds appear as **three distinct ISA instruction names** (`reset`, `measure_reset`, `measure_reset_2`) in
  `isa_instruction_names`; readout error at submission (`properties.json`) **within 1.5x of the same-day snapshot on every qubit** (the 03:08Z
  CSV or a newer one regenerated on), reported as a map; qubits already flagged in the snapshot are reported but do not fail the clause.
- **(iii) locked time per execution within 1.5x** of the estimate: as kill (3) above.

**What the smoke test decides for Paper 2, beyond the gate.** (a) **Reset error scale**: the per-qubit map at 2,048 shots (resolution about
3e-3) says whether Q1's 65,536-shot design is needed as written or a qubit class is already out of H1's 1e-3 per-qubit bound. (b) **MCM
latency and backaction probes**: `measure_reset` / `measure_reset_2` accepted by SamplerV2 at 118 qubits in parallel with one classical bit
each (the `mcm` register returned per shot, `bitarrays.npz` keys `pub<i>__mcm`), their scheduled durations from the target (expected 1,940 /
1,140 ns against the 400 ns native reset) and the `init_qubits` False job returning (the pre-shot state); the Q2 slice (M0, r = 1 and 16, X
readout, both target preparations, reset and delay arms) gives the first spectator <X> under a neighbour's reset against the matched delay,
i.e. the backaction phase and loss scale at r = 16 (about 1.4 mrad per reset resolvable at 2,048 shots against H2's 5 mrad bound), which
decides whether Q2 runs at 16,384 shots or its 8,192-shot fallback. (c) **Frame tracking**: the Q3 slice (`sparse0`, `dense0`, frame 0, m = 1
/ 16 / 64) checks that P(outcome != `expected_z`) on the mask qubits is at the readout floor for every m (the frame-tracked expectation is
right, so the twirled reset is not read as I/2) and gives the spectator per-cycle excess at m = 64 (H3's 1e-3 line; the Deviation 5 echo
flag is switched on for Q3 only if the predicted spectator error exceeds 5e-5). (d) **Data sizes**: the compressed `bitarrays.npz` against
its 1.3 MB raw bound calibrates the Q2 (44.2 MB raw) and Q3 (28.8 MB raw) placement under the 20 MB rule (bundle or Git LFS). (e) **Sampler
acceptance**: reset and mid-circuit `measure_reset` instructions on 118 qubits in one job, `init_qubits` honoured per job, `rep_delay` at the
default, the Batch of two jobs, and the new `--submit-only` / `--retrieve` path for Sampler lists (Section 8), which is this run's first live use.

**Pause / stop rule.** A failing Gate P2-2 clause holds Q1-Q5 until the cause is logged and the PI signs a recorded deviation; a kill
criterion ends the hardware stage (Section 4 of the pre-registration). No minutes are spent on a paused stage.

## 7. Known limits

1. The fake backend's reset (2,232 ns) and measure (2.2 us) durations and its stale 2025 calibration make `sched_ns`, the budget with target
   durations (0.442 / 0.102 min) and the layout check (46, 85) of the dry run unrepresentative; the day's values come from ibm_phoenix's
   target and properties.
2. `measure_reset` / `measure_reset_2` are synthetic stand-ins on the fake; their live durations, multi-bit guard (a target reporting more
   than one classical bit is refused) and Sampler acceptance are first tested by this run.
3. Q7, Q11, Q67 and Q114 (below the Deviation 53 coherence floor) and Q105 (readout 6.5e-2) stay in the parallel arms, flagged: the Paper 2
   pre-registration flags outliers and never drops them from the parallel set. Their Q1 / Q2 / Q3 readings are reported on the maps; H3's mean
   already excludes the readout-flagged qubits, not the coherence-flagged ones (a coherence flag column would be a Paper 2 deviation).
4. The Q4 edges moved on eleven rows between the 20 Sep 03:08Z and the 21 Sep calibrations and on five rows between 01:27Z and 02:05Z; a live
   readout excursion on any of the 24 patch qubits refuses the list. The smoke's Q4 slice is a protocol check only.
5. Kill criterion (3) read on the whole-job charge is dominated by IBM's 3-5 s job floors on a 5.3-s list (Section 2.1); the pre-flight reads
   it per execution as Gate P2-2 words it and logs the whole-job ratio.
6. The compressed size of hardware bit arrays is unknown until this run; the smoke's own arrays stay under 1.5 MB in any case.
7. `--submit-only` and `--retrieve` for Sampler lists were added for this run (`gradvar.paper2.retrieve_sampler_jobs`) and are tested under
   a mocked runtime only; discovery of a Sampler job by signature (`job_id` null in the ids file) is not implemented, so if the submitting run
   dies before writing the ids file the ids are taken from the run log (`submitted job <id>` lines are flushed) and written by hand.
8. `verify_sampler_inputs` compares pub counts and per-pub ISA op counts of the stored inputs against the rebuilt circuits (no observables or
   parameters in a Sampler pub); a decode failure of the stored pubs is recorded and the count match stands.
9. Without `--yes-submit` the runner always dry-runs (the `--dry-run` flag is redundant with `--joblist`).

## 8. Retrieval and the human steps

**Ids file the runner writes** (`gradvar.paper2.execute_sampler_joblist` -> `write_ids_file(ids_file_path(run_root, <UTC date>, jl["name"]))`),
right after the second submission and before any result is awaited:

```
data/runs/<YYYY-MM-DD, UTC date of submission>/dryrun_03_paper2_smoke_job_ids.json
```

(the list's `name` is `dryrun_03_paper2_smoke`, not the file stem `03_paper2_smoke`; the CSV of the retrieval is
`data/jobs/03_paper2_smoke_retrieved_<utc>.csv`). The Action commits it even if the run is later killed (`if: always()`). Both workflows
upload `data/artifacts/` (the qpy) as a 90-day artefact and commit `data/jobs/`, `data/calibrations/`, `data/runs/`; a `bitarrays.npz` above
20 MB would go under `data/lfs/` and is not expected on this run.

### Human steps (Owais), verbatim

**Step 1 - arm (after this pre-flight is posted in the thread and its permalink copied).** In `data/joblists/dryrun/03_paper2_smoke.json`
make exactly two edits, commit to `main`:

```
"dry_run": true,                                              ->   "dry_run": false,
"preflight_review": "TBD: pre-flight review permalink",       ->   "preflight_review": "<Slack permalink of the posted pre-flight message>",
```

(commit message, for example: `arm dryrun/03_paper2_smoke: pre-flight <permalink>`). Nothing else in the file changes; the runner

**Step 2 - dispatch.** GitHub -> Actions -> "run hardware job list" -> Run workflow -> branch `main`; **joblist** =
`data/joblists/dryrun/03_paper2_smoke.json`; **dry_run** ("Build and transpile only; submit nothing") **unticked**; **submit_only** ("Submit,

submits the 2 jobs in one Batch, writes and commits `data/runs/<date>/dryrun_03_paper2_smoke_job_ids.json` and exits. Post the run link in
the thread.

**Step 3 - retrieve (later, when the jobs have run; no polling).** GitHub -> Actions -> "retrieve hardware jobs" -> Run workflow -> branch
`main`; **ids_file** = `data/runs/<date>/dryrun_03_paper2_smoke_job_ids.json` (the path the Step 2 log printed); Run. It commits the 2 bundles
