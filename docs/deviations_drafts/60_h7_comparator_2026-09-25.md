## 4. The note's items 1–4

### Item 1. H7 comparator and guard (must-fix)

- **Cut accumulators** (`gradvar/pauliprop.py`: `propagate_truncated(..., cuts=(ℓ, ...))`, `propagate_sampled(..., cuts=(ℓ, ...))`).
  After the Ry block of layer L − ℓ + 1 (`cut_index`: the first op, in Heisenberg order, of forward layer L − ℓ),
  A<sub>ℓ</sub> = Σ w over the diagonal strings and B<sub>ℓ</sub> = Σ w μ<sub>P</sub>, with μ<sub>P</sub> = Π<sub>q∈P</sub> μ<sub>q</sub> and
  μ<sub>q</sub> = E⟨Z<sub>q</sub>⟩ after forward layer L − ℓ (`layer_mean_z`: the dial's t<sub>z</sub> plus D<sub>z</sub> times the
  relaxation feed of the CZ block, the prefix rule the engine already uses). MSD(ℓ) = E[C²] − 2B<sub>ℓ</sub> + A<sub>ℓ</sub>; the prefix
  identity cancels. The accumulators only read the weights, so a run with cuts is bit-identical to one without (tested for both
  engines). Each kept string contributes w<sub>P</sub>Δ<sub>P</sub> with Δ<sub>P</sub> ≥ (1 − μ<sub>P</sub>)² ≥ 0, so the truncated MSD is
  a lower bound; the sampler is unbiased. μ<sub>P</sub> in product form is exact for the unital base model with a dial, the model of the
  Deviation 46 comparators (derivation and validation: `docs/PAULIPROP.md`, section 'Deviation 60').
- **δ well below the MSD scale.** The Deviation 46 settings truncate at δ = 10<sup>−6</sup> and 10<sup>−7</sup> against
  MSD(2) ≈ 3×10<sup>−3</sup> and MSD(4) ≈ 4×10<sup>−5</sup>; the comparator is the sampled value, and the truncated values (lower bounds)
  enter σ through the deficit term.
- **Bug check:** Section 5. **Comparator:** Section 6, √MSD(2) = 0.05540 ± 1.3×10<sup>−4</sup> on the pinned day-3 placement.
- **σ.** `rms_l2_sigma` is the engine's truncation or sampling error only: the Deviation 15 rule of the H5 / H6 rows
  (`predictions._pp_lookup`: 2σ = max(2 s.e., sampled − truncated)), applied to the RMS. No model-error term is adopted for H5 / H6 in
  this Deviation, so none is added here.
- **Compared quantity: √MSD against the statistic with the shot term subtracted.** Section 3b subtracts the residual pattern term only
  ("the variance over the 256 per-mask differences minus their shot variance, its mean over draws divided by K"), which leaves the shot
  term of the mean difference in the statistic; Section 3 'Pairing' says "Shot and pattern-noise floors are subtracted with their
  uncertainty propagated". This Deviation follows 'Pairing'. Per draw, with diff<sub>m</sub> the K per-mask differences and
  sv<sub>m</sub> their shot variance, the statistic averages mean(diff)² − [Var<sub>m</sub>(diff) − mean(sv)]/K − mean(sv)/K over the
  draws, which estimates MSD(ℓ); the two subtractions add to Var<sub>m</sub>(diff)/K, so the shot model drops out of the point estimate.
  The interval combines the spread over draws with a bootstrap over masks within draws of the subtracted terms, as before. The statistic
  with the shot term kept is reported as `rms_with_shot`; the note puts the shot term at 1.0 % of the RMS at ℓ = 2.
- **Guard.** `evaluate_h7` is not-evaluable when no comparator matches the rows' placement, whatever the ℓ = 4 test shows; when full and
  truncated rows do not pair; and when the rows mix placements. A comparator without `rms_l2_sigma` is named in the verdict's note.

### Item 2. Row mapping and point ids (must-fix)

- **Before.** `_point_id` (`gradvar/analysis/loader.py`) built a reset_dial id from reset kind, p, n, L, k and resilience only:
  `trunc_full_p0.5_L8`, `trunc_l2_p0.5_L8` and the H5 / H6 point `dial_p0.5_L8_kL` all mapped to `reset p0.5 n52 L8 k8 r0`, and
  `evaluate_h7` (kind 'truncation', column `ell`) saw no rows.
- **Effect before the fix, traced.** `estimators.dial_point` keeps rows with finite ev_plus and ev_minus and `point_table` rows with a
  finite gradient; the runner logs an unshifted pub's value as ev_plus with ev_minus and gradient NaN. The H5 / H6 inputs therefore
  dropped the truncation rows, and no H5 / H6 number was affected. The repeat index (grouped by point id and seed) interleaved the full
  and truncated rows, which share seed 23291001 (the dial point has 22991001), and H7 returned not-evaluable.
- **After.** At load time a reset_dial pub with `unshifted` becomes kind `truncation` with `ell` = `truncate_to` (0 for the full
  circuit), read from the bundle's job.json, with a fallback on the probe ids `trunc_full_*` / `trunc_l<ℓ>_*` for rows without a bundle
  description; the point id is `truncation reset p0.5 n52 L8 l<ℓ> r0`. H5 / H6 select the reset_dial rows and are unchanged, also when
  an unshifted row carries a finite ev_minus (tested). The contingent ℓ = 4 probe maps the same way.
- **Pairing.** `truncation_rms` pairs full and truncated rows by draw and `mask_index` (one row per pair, the first in job order); a pair
  whose θ seed or mask seed differs is a pairing error, and H7 is then not-evaluable.
- **Repeat index.** `_repeat_index` groups with `dropna=False`, so pubs without a seed are numbered instead of left NaN (the same line
  crashed `test_loader_on_committed_marrakesh_run`; Section 8).

### Item 3. The rule for ℓ = 4 (must-fix before any ℓ = 4 data)

- **Rule (a).** ℓ = 4 is an upper-bound point by rule, as Deviation 40 does for the k = 1 rows. Its RMS and interval are reported and its
  95 % upper limit is the reported bound, whatever the interval shows. The `at_shot_floor` flag is removed.
- **Why the pre-registration allows it.** H7 pre-registers ℓ = 4 "as an upper-bound point, unless the measured std(C<sub>mix</sub>)
  exceeds 0.1, in which case ... the ℓ = 4 versus ℓ = 2 fall is tested one-sided", and "an ℓ = 4 RMS at the shot floor is consistent
  with H7 and is reported as an upper bound". Rule (a) keeps the upper-bound label in every case and keeps the one-sided test; the
  refutation criteria are unchanged.
- **Expected outcome (provisional).** ℓ = 4 is reported as an upper bound whatever the data show. The predicted MSD(4) = 4.40×10<sup>−5</sup> ±
  8.9×10<sup>−7</sup> (√ = 0.00663) is below one draw's shot term (about 6.7×10<sup>−5</sup>, the note's estimate); over 100 draws with the shot
  term subtracted it sits near the detection limit (z ≈ 2.2–2.6 in the ideal model, lower with noise; the referee's estimate). The
  predicted std(C<sub>mix</sub>) is 0.13516, above 0.1, so the one-sided test is expected to run; the predicted ℓ = 4 RMS is
  0.120 of the ℓ = 2 RMS, so it is expected to pass unless the measurement departs strongly from the prediction.
- **The gate of the one-sided test** is the std over draws of the full circuit's per-draw mean, not floor-subtracted; pattern and shot
  noise inflate it by about 4 % at p = 0.5 (the referee), which does not matter against 0.1. Unchanged.

## 7. Considered and not adopted

- **Rule (b) for ℓ = 4**, a statistical flag (the statistic's lower limit against mean(sv)/K). With the shot term subtracted, the
  ideal-model ℓ = 4 point would be detected about two times in three (z ≈ 2.2–2.6), so the flag would call it an upper bound only about a
  third of the time and the label would depend on the data. The note excludes combining (b) with "expected to stay an upper bound".
- **√(MSD + shot²) against the statistic as Section 3b writes it.** The shot term depends on the measured per-mask values, so the
  comparator would have to be completed with measured quantities, and Section 3 'Pairing' subtracts it. That statistic is reported
  beside (`rms_with_shot`).
- **A model-error term in `rms_l2_sigma`.** Not adopted for H5 / H6, so not added here (the note's item 1).
- **Re-drawing the H5 / H6 p = 0.5 rows on the 23 Sep placement.** Outside this Deviation; Section 8, observation 1.
