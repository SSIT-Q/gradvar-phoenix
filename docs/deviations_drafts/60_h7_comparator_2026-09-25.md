## 4. The note's items 1–4 and the checkpoint review's M5

### Item 1. H7 comparator and guard (must-fix)

- **Cut accumulators** (`gradvar/pauliprop.py`: `propagate_truncated(..., cuts=(ℓ, ...))`, `propagate_sampled(..., cuts=(ℓ, ...))`).
  After the Ry block of layer L − ℓ + 1 (`cut_index`: the first op, in Heisenberg order, of forward layer L − ℓ),
  A<sub>ℓ</sub> = Σ w over the diagonal strings and B<sub>ℓ</sub> = Σ w μ<sub>P</sub>, with μ<sub>P</sub> = Π<sub>q∈P</sub> μ<sub>q</sub> and
  μ<sub>q</sub> = E⟨Z<sub>q</sub>⟩ after forward layer L − ℓ (`layer_mean_z`: the dial's t<sub>z</sub> plus D<sub>z</sub> times the
  relaxation feed of the CZ block, the prefix rule the engine already uses). MSD(ℓ) = E[C²] − 2B<sub>ℓ</sub> + A<sub>ℓ</sub>; the prefix
  identity cancels. The accumulators only read the weights, so a run with cuts is bit-identical to one without (tested for both
  engines). Each kept string contributes w<sub>P</sub>Δ<sub>P</sub> with Δ<sub>P</sub> ≥ (1 − μ<sub>P</sub>)² ≥ 0; pruning before the
  cut removes whole subtrees and pruning after it removes only E[C²] weight, so MSD − MSD<sub>trunc</sub> =
  Σ<sub>P</sub>(w<sub>P</sub> − w<sub>P</sub><sup>trunc</sup>)Δ<sub>P</sub> + Σ<sub>P</sub> w<sub>P</sub><sup>trunc</sup>(F<sub>P</sub> −
  F<sub>P</sub><sup>trunc</sup>) ≥ 0 and the truncated MSD is a lower bound (review O1); the sampler is unbiased. μ<sub>P</sub> in product form is exact for the unital base model with a dial, the model of the
  Deviation 46 comparators (derivation and validation: `docs/PAULIPROP.md`, section 'Deviation 60').
- **δ well below the MSD scale.** The Deviation 46 settings truncate at δ = 10<sup>−6</sup> and 10<sup>−7</sup> against
  MSD(2) ≈ 3×10<sup>−3</sup> and MSD(4) ≈ 4×10<sup>−5</sup>; the comparator is the sampled value, and the truncated values (lower bounds)
  enter σ through the deficit term.
- **Bug check:** Section 5. **Comparator:** Section 6, √MSD(2) = 0.05540 ± 1.3×10<sup>−4</sup> on the pinned day-3 placement.
- **σ.** `rms_l2_sigma` is the engine's truncation or sampling error only: the Deviation 15 rule of the H5 / H6 rows
  (`predictions._pp_lookup`: 2σ = max(2 s.e., sampled − truncated)), applied to the RMS. No model-error term is adopted for H5 / H6 in
  this Deviation, so none is added here.
- **Compared quantity: √MSD against the statistic with the shot term subtracted.** The Section 3b 'Truncation arm' row subtracts the
  residual pattern term only ("the variance over the 256 per-mask differences minus their shot variance, its mean over draws divided by
  K"), which leaves the shot term of the mean difference in the statistic; the Section 3b 'Analysis' Pairing bullet says "Shot and
  pattern-noise floors are subtracted with their uncertainty propagated". This Deviation follows the Pairing bullet. Per draw, with
  diff<sub>m</sub> the K per-mask differences and
  sv<sub>m</sub> their shot variance, the statistic averages mean(diff)² − [Var<sub>m</sub>(diff) − mean(sv)]/K − mean(sv)/K over the
  draws, which estimates MSD(ℓ); the two subtractions add to Var<sub>m</sub>(diff)/K, so the shot model drops out of the point estimate.
  The statistic with the shot term kept is reported as `rms_with_shot`; the note puts the shot term at 1.0 % of the RMS at ℓ = 2.
- **Interval (review M3).** The paired bootstrap over draws of Section 3b 'Analysis': 10,000 seeded resamples of the draws, each
  selected draw entering as mean(diff)² minus one of its 2,000 mask-bootstrap replicates of Var<sub>m</sub>(diff)/K (the same mask
  positions for the full and the truncated rows); the RMS limits are the roots of the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles, and the kurtosis of
  y<sub>d</sub> is reported beside them (review O4). The first draft's interval was a normal approximation. The review's text takes a mask
  replicate of the whole y<sub>d</sub>; over K masks drawn with replacement the resampled square exceeds mean(diff)² by
  Var<sub>m</sub>(diff)/K on average, so that replicate is centred on mean(diff)² and adds the subtracted terms back. The mask stage
  therefore resamples the subtracted term only, as the pre-registration words it ("a bootstrap over masks within draws for the
  pattern-noise term"); Section 10, M3.
- **Guard.** `evaluate_h7` is not-evaluable when no comparator matches the rows' placement (the placed qubit set, which the comparator
  must record, and patch, edge, n, p, L), whatever the ℓ = 4 test shows; when full and truncated rows do not pair; and when the rows mix
  placements: different point keys, placed qubit sets or, where the bundles record them, broken-coupler sets (review M4; the loader now
  keeps `broken_edges`). A comparator without `rms_l2_sigma` is named in the verdict's note.

## 7. Considered and not adopted

- **Rule (b) for ℓ = 4**, a statistical flag (the statistic's lower limit against mean(sv)/K). With the shot term subtracted, the
  ideal-model ℓ = 4 point would be detected about two times in three (z ≈ 2.2–2.6), so the flag would call it an upper bound only about a

  beside (`rms_with_shot`).
- **A model-error term in `rms_l2_sigma`.** Not adopted for H5 / H6, so not added here (the note's item 1).
- **Keeping the interval comparison for the one-sided ℓ = 4 test** (the review's alternative for M2): not taken; the paired bootstrap is
