## 3. Cause, as far as the repository can establish it

1. **Proximate cause (established).** IBM re-measured readout at about 11:05:20Z and published the result at 11:35:39Z. The package
   was placed, checked and dispatched (11:11 to 11:13Z) on the 09:07:11Z update, whose readout values were dated 9 Nov 04:54Z,
   about 30 hours old. Nothing available to the same-day procedure at dispatch showed the new values.
2. **Why no check fired (established; review 06 defects D1 to D3, review 07 defect E2).** (a) The same-day procedure keys on the
   update time alone, not on the age of the figures inside the update. (b) The Gate 2 (e) evaluator and the Deviation 62 (iv)
   post-run check both read the properties at job creation, so neither can see an update published while a job waits or runs.
   (c) No bundle field records when a job's circuits executed. (d) Block C waited about 1 h 40 min in the queue (about 1,100 jobs
   pending on the device) and the replication `L0` job's window was 83 minutes, both comparable to the usual interval between
   IBM updates (about 2 to 2.5 h, measured on 67 committed snapshots), so a job dispatched on a fresh snapshot can still run
   under a later calibration.
3. **Physical cause of the readout change on Q31 and Q98 (not established).** Q31 read 6.519e-02 in the 11:05Z-dated figures and
   8.789e-03 in the next set, so its excursion did not persist; Q98 read 6.396e-02 and then 1.306e-01, so its excursion grew.
   Gate, T1 and T2 figures are unchanged between the 09:07:11Z and 11:35:39Z sets. The repository holds no measurement of ours
   that says what state those qubits were in while our circuits executed.

## 5. Effect on the two results (stated, not corrected for)

- Readout error on a qubit outside the observable does not enter the Z_75 Z_85 estimator. The observable qubits' level-0 readout
  folding (a_75 a_85)^2 is 0.95108 on the 09:07:11Z set, 0.96149 on the 11:35:39Z set and 0.95765 on the 13:37:31Z set (+1.1 and
  +0.7 percent against the set the predictions were drawn on).
- What a qubit with a 6 to 13 percent readout error does to the gates on it is not in any committed figure. Four such or
  over-cut qubits sit inside the L = 8 and L = 10 cones. No prediction on the 11:35:39Z properties exists.
- Each comparison of 10 Nov is therefore a measurement under one calibration against a prediction under an earlier one, and the
  repository cannot say how much of any measured-to-predicted difference that accounts for. Block C has no registered decision
  test. The replication's registered decision ("not replicated: the flag closes") stands with note N1.

## 6. What lifts the pause (proposed; for the PI to accept, change or refuse)

1. The PI signs this record (Section 8 of the pre-registration: reason, date, initials).
2. Before the next dispatch, the execution-time calibration rule (draft Deviation 65) is adopted, so that (a) Gate 2 (e) and the
   Deviation 62 (iv) post-run check are evaluated on the properties in force when each job ran, not at its creation, (b) each job's
   running and finished times are recorded with the bundle, and (c) each job is read against predictions drawn on the calibration in
   force when it ran, by pre-committed code. Until it is adopted nothing is dispatched.
3. The next package is placed on a fresh same-day snapshot under the unchanged rule (Deviation 62); a placement that puts a rung
   on a qubit over a cut is refused by the live layout check, as now. Whether Q98 and Q31 are placeable is decided by that
   snapshot, not by this record.
4. If Gate 2 (e), evaluated as in item 2, meets the pause condition again on the next run, the campaign pauses again and the
   PI is asked whether the clause itself should change. This record does not pre-authorise that change.

## 7. Considered and not adopted

- **Reading the event as post-run (no pause), as on day 2.** Not adopted: the figures were dated before dispatch and published
  before most of the circuit time; the clause's condition is met on the figures in force during the run.
- **Voiding or re-measuring the two lists.** Not adopted: no registered rule voids a measurement for a later calibration, the
  registered decision of the replication does not depend on the four qubits' readout, and re-measuring after seeing the values
  would be a choice made with knowledge of the result.
- **Re-reading the points against predictions on the 11:35:39Z figures now.** Not adopted here: that is the subject of draft
  Deviation 65 and must be done by pre-committed code and a worker who has not seen the measured values.
- **Narrowing the clause's qubit set to the L = 2 cone**, on which the condition is not met. Not adopted: Deviation 52 (ii) and
  pre-flight 09 fix the set as the cone qubits of the measured patches, which at L = 8 and L = 10 is all 82 placed qubits.
