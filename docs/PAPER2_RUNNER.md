# Paper 2 runner: the SamplerV2 path and the Q1-Q5 job lists

Implements the hardware side of the Paper 2 pre-registration ("Reset and mid-circuit-measurement characterisation of
ibm_phoenix", v0.4.4, 20 Sep 2026, Deviations 1-7): a `primitive: "sampler"` job-list format for `gradvar.hardware`, the
circuit builders for Q1-Q5 in `gradvar/paper2.py`, the production lists `data/joblists/paper2/Q1..Q5.json`, the shared
smoke list `data/joblists/dryrun/03_paper2_smoke.json` (converted from the EstimatorV2 estimate), and
`tests/test_paper2_sampler.py`. Every list carries `dry_run: true` and the placeholder `preflight_review`; nothing
submits until both change after the pre-flight review, exactly as for the Paper 1 lists. The lists are generated by
`scripts/make_paper2_joblists.py` and each must equal its output on the snapshot it names (tested per list): Q1-Q5 on
`ibm_phoenix_2026-09-20T030813Z.csv` until their own pre-flight (Deviation 8: days 4-5), the smoke list on
`ibm_phoenix_2026-09-21T022722Z.csv` (the 21 Sep 02:05Z calibration, `docs/preflight/07_paper2_smoke_2026-09-21.md`;
`--lists dryrun/03_paper2_smoke.json` regenerates it alone). Regenerate each list from its run day's newest committed
calibration before its pre-flight.

## 1. Job-list format (`primitive: "sampler"`)

The Estimator fields keep their meaning (`backend`, `instance`, `dry_run`, `preflight_review`, `notes`, `budget`,
`layout_check`, `rep_delay_probe`; `points` stays present and empty). New fields:

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `primitive` | `"sampler"` (the default `"estimator"` keeps the Paper 1 path untouched) |
| `protocol` | `smoke`, `Q1` ... `Q5`: the stage; the CSV `stage` column and `job.json` `protocol` |
| `init_qubits` | list default for `SamplerV2.options.execution.init_qubits` (pre-registration Section 3: True except the one Q1 job) |
| `sampler_jobs[]` | one SamplerV2 job per entry (Section 3 "Job packing": one job per stage; Q1 has three under Deviation 6). Fields: `id` (job tag, unique), `shots`, `init_qubits` (overrides the list default), `rep_delay_us` (optional, Deviation 1: sets `options.execution.rep_delay`, budgeted at that value in both columns), `purpose`, `circuits` (generator specs below) |
| `qubit_set` | resolved from the named snapshot by `gradvar.paper2.operational_qubits`: `qubits` (the 118 parallel qubits), `separate` (qubit 79, Deviation 6), `excluded` (17), `flagged_readout` (above 3e-2 on 20 Sep 03:08Z: 24, 55, 62, 67, 73, 77, 107), `flagged_cz_cluster` (55, 61, 62, 63, 72, 73; a separate column, Deviation 7 (iv)), `snapshot` |
| `q4_patches` | the twelve row edges of Q4 (`edges`), the exclusion set used (Deviation 22 set plus 79), `backtracked` (section 3) and the per-row candidates; **authoritative for the run** |
| `randomness` | `seed` 20260919, Q3 dense-mask count / probability / frame count, Q4 masks per p and the p list, and the SHA-256 of every Q3 mask |

The runner re-derives `qubit_set` and `q4_patches` from `qubit_set.snapshot` (in `data/calibrations/`) at build time and
refuses a list that no longer matches (regenerate with `python scripts/make_paper2_joblists.py --snapshot <csv>`).

### Circuit specs (`sampler_jobs[].circuits[]`)

A spec expands deterministically (`gradvar.paper2.expand_spec`) into circuits; each spec may set `protocol` (the smoke
list runs Q1-Q4 circuits under stage `smoke`).

| kind | fields and defaults | circuits |
|---|---|---|
| `q1` | `arm` a-f (default all; `d0` / `d1` pick one of the two (d) circuits), `reset_kind` for a / b / f (default reset, measure_reset, measure_reset_2), `m` for c (default 2, 4), `prep` for d (default "1", "0"), `qubits` `"parallel"` (default, 118 qubits) or `"separate"` (qubit 79; labels get `_q79`) | 14 for the full set; `arm: "b"` gives 3; `reset_kind: "reset"` gives the 8-circuit Q5 subset; the Deviation 6 job is `qubits: "separate", arm: [a, b, f], reset_kind: reset` (3) |
| `q2` | product of `masks` 0-4, `reps` 1 / 4 / 16, `axes` X / Y / Z, `target_prep` "0" / "1", `arms` reset / delay | 180 |
| `q3` | product of `masks` (names `sparse0..4`, `dense0..7`), `frames` 0-3, `cycles` 1 / 16 / 64; `echo` (bool, default false; Deviation 5 / 7 (vi), label suffix `_echo`) | 156 |
| `q4` | product of `p` 0.25 / 0.5, `masks` 0-15, `inputs` 0 / 1 / + / +i, `axes` X / Y / Z | 384 |

## 2. What each protocol builds (`gradvar.paper2.build_circuit`)

All circuits live on the full 120-qubit register with the identity `initial_layout` (no two-qubit gate anywhere, so no
routing); register `meas` holds the terminal readout, bit *i* being `measured_qubits[i]`, and register `mcm` (present
only when the reset instruction carries a classical bit) the outputs of measurement-based resets, bit *i* being
`mcm_qubits[i]`. Native reset is `qc.reset`; `delay` is 400 ns (2140 ns on qubit 79, `reset_ns`); `measure_reset` and
`measure_reset_2` are taken from `backend.target` on ibm_phoenix (one classical bit in the 2026-09-19 configuration; a
target reporting two or more is refused with an explicit error) and, on a fake target that lacks them, replaced by
synthetic one-qubit one-clbit stand-ins whose definition is measure + reset (listed in `synthetic_target_instructions`;
`sched_ns` is then None because the stand-in has no duration).

* **Q1** (Section 2 Q1): on the 118 parallel qubits, (a) X, kind, measure; (b) kind, measure; (c) X, reset x m, measure
  (m = 2, 4); (d) X, measure and measure alone; (e) X, delay, measure; (f) H, kind, H, measure (the |+> arm read in X).
  Job `Q1-init_true` = the 14 circuits with `init_qubits` True; `Q1-init_false` = arm (b) with `init_qubits` False;
  `Q1-q79` (Deviation 6) = native arms (a), (b), (f) on qubit 79 alone, its 2140 ns reset padding nothing else.
* **Q2** (Section 2 Q2): masks M_s = {q = 10 i + j : (i + 2 j) mod 5 = s}; `q2_roles` gives targets (mask members in the
  parallel set: 22 in M_0, which holds both 17 and 79, Deviation 7 (ii)), the 422 directed pairs (428 minus the six at
  qubit 79), spectators (pair neighbours not above the readout cut), `excluded_spectators`, and the controls
  (non-targets without a target neighbour, including the neighbours of 17 and 79). Non-targets are prepared in |+> for
  the X and Y readouts and in |0> for Z; the target gets X when `target_prep` is "1"; r repetitions of reset or delay(400
  ns) act on every target; non-targets are rotated to the axis; every parallel qubit is read. Deviation 4 (tau_eff fit)
  needs no builder change: both preps x both arms are in the list and `delay_ns_by_target` is logged.
* **Q3** (Section 2 Q3): reset qubits X-prepared, spectators H-prepared; m cycles of [Pauli P_q on every qubit, reset on
  the mask and delay(400 ns) on the spectators, P_q again]; spectators H before readout. Per-cycle spectator idle 480 ns
  (two 40 ns Pauli layers, Deviation 7 (vi)). With `echo: true` the spectator idle becomes delay(200), X, delay(200) and
  the closing Pauli is X P (I, X, Y, Z -> X, I, Z, Y up to phase), so the noiseless outcome is unchanged; the flag is
  logged per circuit (`echo`, `spectator_idle_ns`, `echo_delay_ns`) and in the CSV. Frames: `(4, 64, 118)` Paulis drawn
  after the eight dense masks from one `default_rng(20260919)` over the 118 parallel qubits; the m-cycle circuit uses the
  first m cycles of its frame (Deviation 7 (v)). `expected_z` per reset qubit is 1 when the final cycle's Pauli is X or Y,
  else 0; stored per circuit (`expected_z`, `expected_z_string`) and in the CSV. Masks `sparse0..4` are the Q2 masks
  (sparse0 has 22 qubits); `dense0..7` are Bernoulli(0.5) over the parallel set.
* **Q4** (Section 2 Q4): twelve horizontal edges (section 3), both qubits prepared in the input state, reset on the masked
  qubit(s) and delay(400 ns) on the other(s) (the time-matched I branch), axis rotation, readout of the 24 patch qubits.
  Masks: for each p the per-patch pattern list (00 x 9, 10 x 3, 01 x 3, 11 x 1 at p = 0.25; 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 at p = 0.5) is
  permuted with the shared rng (p = 0.25 first), so `p_hat` = p exactly on every patch qubit.
* **Q5**: the eight native-reset Q1 circuits over the 118 parallel qubits at 32,768 shots (qubit 79 is not in Q5); the
  list describes one day and is dispatched on each of the four days (regenerate it from that day's snapshot).

## 3. Qubit sets from the calibration snapshot

`gradvar.paper2.operational_qubits(csv)`: operational, T1 and T2 present, readout error below 0.5, minus the
pre-registered dead qubit 17 and minus `SEPARATE_QUBITS = (79,)` (Deviation 6) -> 118 parallel qubits + [79] on
`ibm_phoenix_2026-09-20T030813Z.csv`. Readout flags above 3e-2 and the CZ cluster are recorded in separate columns and
kept (Section 3, Deviation 7 (iv)); the readout flags drive Q2's spectator exclusion at build time.

`gradvar.paper2.q4_row_edges(csv, exclusion)` (Deviation 7 (i)): the exclusion set is `gradvar.noise.exclusion_from_calibration`
with the snapshot's raw properties (`gradvar.hardware.properties_for_csv`), that is the pre-registered cut plus Paper 1's
Deviation 22 rule (|ZZ| >= 1 MHz to an excluded qubit, init error >= 5e-4; on this snapshot 8, 18, 27, 59, 91, 119
among others), plus qubit 79. Per row the admissible horizontal edges are ordered by (summed readout error, column) and
the assignment is the first complete one in depth-first order with a left-column distance of at least three between
consecutive rows, backtracking to the previous row's next-best edge when a row has none. The greedy rule is infeasible
on the 19-20 Sep snapshots: row 5's best edge 56-57 (column 6) leaves row 6 (61, 62, 63 excluded) with no admissible
edge, so row 5 takes 57-58 (`q4_patches.backtracked`). Result on 20 Sep 03:08Z: 0-1, 15-16, 20-21, 38-39, 42-43, 57-58,
64-65, 70-71, 85-86, 92-93, 108-109, 115-116 (rows 7-10 differ from the 19 Sep placement because 91 and 119 joined the
exclusion set and 78-79 is out under Deviation 6).

Live re-check (`gradvar.paper2.sampler_layout_check`, Paper 1 Deviation 26 via `gradvar.hardware.layout_check`, no
couplers): on the submitting path a used qubit above the readout or init-error cut is **flagged and kept**
(`flagged_live_qubits`, action `submit-flagged`; Section 3 flags outliers on the maps, it does not drop them); a
non-operational used qubit or a failing Q4 patch qubit **refuses** before any job is created, and `layout_check:
"override"` is never accepted for a Q4 patch qubit (the pair is the observable). Dry runs log the check (`enforced:
false`; FakeNighthawk's stale calibration fails 46 and 85, which is why the dry-run output says `fail ... action logged`).

## 4. Budget (model v2 for the Sampler)

`gradvar.paper2.estimate_budget_sampler`, reached through `gradvar.hardware.estimate_budget` for `primitive: "sampler"`:
`T_job = 2 s + sum over circuits of shots x (rep_delay + gate length + t_meas + 10 us)` with the TREX term identically
zero (resilience 0, no twirling) and `t_meas` counted once per terminal readout plus each measurement-based reset's own
target duration (`mcm_executions` counts them). Gate length per circuit from the spec (`circuit_gate_us`): 40 ns per
single-qubit gate layer, measure_reset 1.94 us, measure_reset_2 1.14 us, native reset and its matched delay at
`max(reset_ns)` over the circuit's reset qubits (0.40 us everywhere except 2.14 us for the qubit-79 job; review S3), a
Q3 cycle at 480 ns (520 ns with the echo); a fake backend's target durations replace the ibm_phoenix figures in
`budget_estimate_with_target_durations`.

| list | jobs | circuits | executions | at 250 us | at 1 us | v0.4.4 Section 3 table |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `paper2/Q1.json` (incl. `Q1-q79`) | 3 | 20 | 1,310,720 | 5.85 min | 24.5 s | 5.0 + 0.9 min / 20 + 3 s |
| `paper2/Q2.json` | 1 | 180 | 2,949,120 | 13.05 min | 48.6 s | 13.1 min / 49 s |
| `paper2/Q3.json` | 1 | 156 | 1,916,928 | 8.82 min | 51.8 s | 8.8 min / 52 s |
| `paper2/Q4.json` | 1 | 384 | 786,432 | 3.47 min | 12.5 s | 3.5 min / 13 s |
| `paper2/Q5.json` (one day, x 4) | 1 | 8 | 262,144 (x 4) | 1.18 min (4.72) | 5.5 s (22) | 4.7 min / 23 s |
| `dryrun/03_paper2_smoke.json` | 2 | 40 | 81,920 | 0.43 min | 5.3 s | 0.43 min / 5 s (Deviation 7 (iii)) |
| **campaign** | | | **8,093,696** | **36.34 min** | **2.75 min** | 36.3 min / 2.7 min, cap 45 (tolerance 1.24x) |

`tests/test_paper2_sampler.py::test_budgets_match_the_preregistration_table` asserts the executions exactly, the 250 us
total to 1 percent, the per-list figures, the formula per job, the zero TREX term and the 45-minute cap. The 1 us total
(2.75 min) is 1.8 percent above the table's 2.7 min because the table's seconds column omits the 2 s job charge for the
smoke test and for the qubit-79 job (3 s each there, 5.3 s and 5.0 s with the charge); the test allows 2 percent there.

## 5. Running

```
python -m gradvar.hardware --joblist data/joblists/paper2/Q1.json                 # dry run on FakeNighthawk, bundles + CSV
python -m gradvar.hardware --joblist data/joblists/dryrun/03_paper2_smoke.json --simulate --simulate-shots 256
python -m gradvar.hardware --joblist data/joblists/paper2/Q4.json --budget         # the budget JSON
python scripts/make_paper2_joblists.py [--snapshot data/calibrations/<csv>] [--lists dryrun/03_paper2_smoke.json]   # regenerate (a subset of) the lists
python -m gradvar.hardware --joblist <armed list> --yes-submit --submit-only       # Action: submit, write the ids file, exit (long queues)
python -m gradvar.hardware --retrieve data/runs/<UTC date>/<list name>_job_ids.json   # Action: collect the bundles and CSV rows later
```

`--simulate` executes the dry run's ISA circuits on `AerSimulator(method="stabilizer")` (every Paper 2 circuit is
Clifford) so the result files are exercised at 120 qubits; synthetic `measure_reset` stand-ins are decomposed through
their definitions. Submission goes through the same `--yes-submit` flow, refusals (`dry_run`, placeholder
`preflight_review`, missing instance secret, stale budget, instance plan) and `Batch` as the Estimator path, then one
`SamplerV2` job per `sampler_jobs` entry with `options.default_shots`, `execution.init_qubits`, twirling and dynamical
decoupling off, and `execution.rep_delay` only when the job sets `rep_delay_us`. Right after the last submission and
before any result is awaited the job ids go to `data/runs/<UTC date>/<list name>_job_ids.json` (the Paper 1 ids-file
schema, `gradvar.hardware.write_ids_file`, one entry per sampler job with `tag` = the job id, `level` 0, `shots`, `pubs`
= circuits, `init_qubits`); `--submit-only` returns there, and `--retrieve <ids file>` (`gradvar.paper2.retrieve_sampler_jobs`,
reached from `gradvar.hardware.retrieve_jobs` after the same instance checks) rebuilds the circuits from the list and its
named snapshot, checks them against the stored `job.inputs` (`verify_sampler_inputs`: pub count, per-pub ISA op counts),
and writes the same bundles (properties at the job's creation time, the Paper 2 layout check recomputed from them,
`bitarrays.npz`, `counts.json`, `usage()`) and CSV rows (`data/jobs/<list stem>_retrieved_<utc>.csv`, notes `retrieved`)
as the live path, under the job's creation day. Discovery of a Sampler job by signature (`job_id` null) is not
implemented: fill the id in from the run log.

### Bundle (`data/runs/<date>/<job_id>/`) and data policy (Deviation 7 (vii))

Same files as the Estimator bundle except `circuits.qpy`: `job.json`, `result.json`, `metadata.json`, `options.json`,
`properties.json`, `target.json`, `circuits.json`, with `job_kind: "sampler"`, `primitive`, `protocol`, `sampler_job`,
`init_qubits`, `simulated`, `qubit_set` (with `separate`), `q4_patches`, `randomness`, the Paper 2 `layout_check` (with
`separate_qubits`) and, per circuit under `points`, the logging-schema fields (`reset_kind`, `mask_id`, `mask_hash`,
`frame_id`, `reps`, `prep`, `meas_axis`, `expected_z`, `echo`), the qubit roles, `qubit_group`, `registers` (register ->
qubit list), `sched_ns` (scheduled circuit duration from the target), `reset_ns_target` and `synthetic_target_instructions`.

* **ISA circuits are not committed for Sampler jobs** (one Q3 m = 64 circuit is 1.3 MB of qpy; the Q3 job would be about
  84 MB): `gradvar.hardware._qpy_policy` writes `circuits.qpy` under `data/artifacts/<date>/<job_id>/` (git-ignored; the
  Action uploads `data/artifacts/` with `upload-artifact`, 90-day retention) and records `circuits_qpy` in job.json:
  SHA-256, size, circuit count, qiskit / qiskit-ibm-runtime / qpy versions and the path. The circuits are deterministic
  from the list, the snapshot, the seed and the qiskit version. Estimator bundles keep `circuits.qpy` as before.
* **Per-shot bit arrays**: when a result exists, `bitarrays.npz` (compressed packed bits of every register of every pub,
  keys `pub<i>__<register>` and `__num_bits`) is placed by `gradvar.hardware.place_large_file`: in the bundle when under
  20 MB, else under `data/lfs/<date>/<job_id>/` (track with `git lfs track "data/lfs/**"` before committing) and flagged;
  `job.json["bitarrays"]` and `counts.json["bitarrays"]` carry path, size, SHA-256 and `in_bundle`. The Q2 job's array
  is 44 MB raw and compresses to roughly 6-40 MB depending on how random the readouts are, so it may land in `lfs/`.
* **Always committed**: `counts.json` (per register: shots, bits, qubit list, per-bit P(1), distinct outcomes, and the
  counts dict when there are at most 1024 distinct outcomes), `job.json`, and `data/jobs/<name>_<utc>.csv` with one row
  per circuit (`gradvar.paper2.SAMPLER_LOG_COLUMNS`, the Section 5 schema: `stage`, `protocol`, `label`, `reset_kind`,
  `mask_id`, `mask_hash`, `frame_id`, `reps`, `prep`, `meas_axis`, `expected_z`, `echo`, `shots`, `rep_delay_submitted`,
  `init_qubits`, `sched_ns`, `reset_ns`, `counts_path`, `qpu_seconds`, ...).

In `circuits.json` the Estimator-era field `mid_circuit_measures` counts every ISA `measure`, terminal readout included
(Sampler circuits read out, Estimator pubs do not): kill rule (c) reads as `ops["measure"] > n_measured`.

## 6. Pre-registration points raised by the runner and their disposition

The eight ambiguities listed by the first build were resolved in v0.4.4 (Deviations 6-7, adopted 20 Sep 2026 under
delegated authority; countersignature on return): qubit 79 isolated (6); Q4 edge rule made depth-first with backtracking
on the Deviation 22 set plus 79 (7 (i)); M_0 has 22 acting targets (7 (ii)); the smoke test is two jobs sharing 40
circuits (7 (iii)); the CZ-cluster flag is a separate column and H3's "non-flagged" means not readout-flagged (7 (iv));
Q3 frames nest (7 (v)); the Q3 cycle idle is 480 ns and the optional echo is specified (7 (vi)); the data policy above
(7 (vii)). Deviation 4 withdrew the fixed 0.9375 coefficient (tau_eff is fitted), so nothing about qubit 79's delay
enters the Q2 analysis any more. Still open for the analysis stage, not the runner:

1. The 1 us seconds column of the Section 3 table omits the 2 s job charge for the smoke test and the qubit-79 job (about
   4 s of the 165 s total; section 4).
2. Q5 runs over the 118 parallel qubits; whether qubit 79's daily drift is wanted (a fourth 2 s job per day, about 0.5
   min over the four days) is not stated in Deviation 6, which names Q1-Q4 only.
3. The FakeNighthawk dry run fails the layout check on 46 and 85 (stale fake calibration); which of them is a Q4 patch
   qubit depends on the placement (85-86 on the 20 Sep 03:08Z snapshot, 46-47 on the 21 Sep 02:05Z calibration of the
   smoke list), and a live failure on a patch qubit refuses the list and requires regenerating it.

## 7. Tests

`tests/test_paper2_sampler.py`: every list validates, budgets match, refuses to submit; budgets vs the v0.4.4 Section 3
table (8,093,696 executions, 36.3 min to 1 percent, cap 45) and the per-job formula, the qubit-79 job at 0.9 min, echo
and separate-job gate lengths; qubit sets with 79 separated, Q2 mask properties (24 per mask by formula, distance >= 3,
422 directed pairs once, M_0 with 22 acting targets), Q3 randomness over 118 qubits, Q4 stratified counts and `p_hat` =
p, the depth-first row-edge rule and its recorded backtrack, stale-list and other-snapshot refusal; spec expansion counts
(separate group, `d0` / `d1`, echo labels); schema validation (separate qubits, echo type); a dry run of every list
(bundle layout without qpy, artefact placement and hashes, options read-back, registers, `expected_z`, reset / delay
counts, CSV schema with `echo`); `--simulate` with per-shot capture of `meas` and `mcm`, `bitarrays` placement records
and the noiseless protocol outcomes; the echo builder (X-folded Pauli, unchanged noiseless outcomes, doubled delays);
`place_large_file`; the multi-clbit guard of `reset_operation`; the submitting branch under a mocked runtime (flags
kept, Q4 refusal, override denied, non-operational refusal, `rep_delay_us` read-back, qpy in the artefact dir); the
`run_joblist` submit dispatch (`--submit-only` reaches the Sampler path with `wait=False`); `--submit-only` writing the
ids file and `--retrieve` completing the bundles, rows and inputs verification from it under a mocked runtime and
service; and that every committed list equals the generator's output on the snapshot it names (`--lists` subset write).
