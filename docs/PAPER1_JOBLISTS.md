# Paper 1 production job lists

The eleven lists under `data/joblists/paper1/` implement the hardware stage of the Paper 1 pre-registration
(`preregistration_q1`, **v0.11.4**, 20 Sep 2026): Section 2 (design), Section 3b (reset dial), Section 5 (Gate 2),
Section 6 (minute budget) and Deviations 17, 18, 22, 24, 26, 27, 28–30, 33–45. They are generated by
`scripts/make_paper1_joblists.py`, budgeted with `gradvar.hardware.estimate_budget` (model v2, Deviation 24), dry-run on
FakeNighthawk, and tested in `tests/test_paper1_joblists.py` (the committed files must equal the generator's output).
Every list carries `dry_run: true` and `preflight_review: "TBD: pre-flight review permalink"`; nothing submits until a
pre-flight review is posted and both fields change in the same commit (`data/joblists/README.md`, Flow).

```
python scripts/make_paper1_joblists.py                      # from data/calibrations/ibm_phoenix_2026-09-20T030813Z.csv
python scripts/make_paper1_joblists.py --snapshot data/calibrations/ibm_phoenix_<run-day>.csv   # run-day regeneration
python scripts/make_paper1_joblists.py --check              # committed lists == generator output
python scripts/make_paper1_joblists.py --m-rule dev17 --out /tmp/p1_dev17   # Deviation 17 M for the budget comparison
python -m gradvar.hardware --joblist data/joblists/paper1/grid_n20.json      # dry run (fake backend)
python -m gradvar.hardware --joblist data/joblists/paper1/dial_arm.json --dry-run-sample 8   # sampled dry run of a 400k-circuit list
```

the rule rather than the instance: it picks the intact 4x10 coupler whose L = 2 cone graph equals the 4x5 rung's, which is

to `interior_edge` otherwise, and records which branch fired (`edge_rule`). Flagged as an ambiguity in section 7.

## 2. Contents

Conventions: k is the 1-based layer of the differentiated Ry, on the first qubit of the observable edge (Section 2, Gradient);
draw d of a point uses seed + d; seeds are deterministic blocks of 1000 per (rung, role, depth, k) from `SEED_BASE = 20261001`
(`make_paper1_joblists.seed_for`), so no two points share a draw unless by design: resilience 0 and 1 of one point share their
draws (paired H4 ratio), the four k of one sweep depth share theirs (paired layer-index ratio), the dial probes with the same
seed are paired draw by draw and mask by mask (Section 3b, Pairing). 4096 shots per shifted circuit; 16384 for the n100 L = 8
headline points and the Gate 1b references. Every job holds at most 300 circuits (`max_experiments`), consecutive pubs of a
group packed together (see `data/joblists/README.md`, job packing).

## 6. Runner changes made for these lists (`gradvar/hardware.py`)

- **`max_experiments` job packing** (`MAX_EXPERIMENTS = 300`, `chunk_by_circuits`, `chunk_tags`): every job group is split in build
  order into jobs of at most 300 circuits, tags `L0`, `L0-j2`, ...; `budget_jobs` packs identically, so the budget's job count is
  the one charged. `job.json` records `max_experiments`.
- **Edge override** (`resolve_edge`): a point's or probe's `edge` may be any intact coupler of the placed patch (Deviation 36);
  `GridPoint.edge`, `hea_observable(patch, edge)`.
- **`null_control` with `L: 0`** (Deviation 43): SPAM-only circuit, one pub per draw with a (2, 0) bindings array; `k` and
  `null_qubit` refused.
- **`reset_dial`**: `M` draws (theta from seed + d, mask m from seed + d + 1 + m), `mask_p` (lottery probability distinct from
  the logged channel strength `p`), `unshifted` (one circuit at θ: the cost `C_mix`), `truncate_to` (last ℓ layers with their θ
  and masks, |0⟩ start), `reset_kind: dephase` (virtual Z + 400 ns delay; timed as `delay` in the budget, so the reported
  `dial_durations_us` dictionary is unchanged and the committed Paper 2 budgets still match their generator).
- **Transpile cache** in `build_pubs`: the parametrised circuit of a point is transpiled once and bound per draw (a grid list
  builds in seconds instead of one transpile per draw).
- **`--dry-run-sample N`** (dry run only, refused with `--yes-submit`): builds only the first N pubs of every job group
  (`build_probes(max_pubs_per_probe=N)`, the first N draws per resilience level of the points) and records
  `dry_run_sample` with the full pub counts in `job.json`; a full build of `dial_arm` (409,603 distinct transpiles) is
  neither needed for the check nor affordable on the reviewer's machine.
- Cherry-picked from `origin/analysis-p1`: `daed931` "hardware: null_control probe kind (candidate Deviation 43)" (the
  analysis pipeline itself is not on main yet).

## 7. Pre-registration ambiguities and gaps found while building (not edited; for the Deviations table)

1. **Ladder counts and placements move with the snapshot.** Section 2 / Deviation 18 record 20 / 39 / 53 / 70 / 87 on the
   19 Sep snapshot; the newest snapshot gives 20 / 37 / 50 / 68 / 85 with the 6x10 and 8x10 at new origins and edges (43_44,
   75_85). The Gate 1 / Gate 1b predictions were drawn on the 19 Sep placements. The text says the placement is re-derived on
   the run day and analyses use the actual n, but does not say whether the predictions are re-drawn per run-day placement or
   the campaign is held to the 19 Sep placements (moving patches when a qubit fails, "the observable edge is not moved").
2. **Deviation 36's edge (93, 103)** is stated as an instance, not a rule; on the 20 Sep snapshot it has hole 91 in its L = 2 cone
   and no longer matches the 4x5 cone. The generator applies the rule (identical cone graph → 94_104 here). Needs a one-line
   reading in the Deviations table.
3. **Three circuits per draw.** Section 2 books "three circuits per draw (θ ± π/2 and the unshifted θ)" and a fourth at θ + π on
   5 draws; the runner builds the shifted pair only (the pre-registered budgets count 600 circuits per point, the lists 400).
   Var[C] and the rotation-survival identity of Section 2 are therefore not measured by these lists unless the runner gains an
   unshifted circuit; the main-grid line has the room (section 3, reading ii).
4. **M for the main grid.** Section 2 says M = 200; Deviation 17 raises it to 400 / 700 "where the shot budget allows"; Section 6
   makes the raise the first call on the surplus and Gate 2 the point of the final recompute. Booked at 200 here; the raise costs
   172 min at 1 µs (fits the line only with two circuits per draw).
5. **Dial resilience level.** Section 3b quotes the dial at resilience 0 and adds "resilience 1 adds only 32 × 16 learning
   executions per job"; kill rule (d) is stated at "the resilience level used on the Paper 1 grid" (0 and 1). The lists book the
   dial at resilience 0 (the quoted budget).
6. **Delay-matched reference timing.** Section 3b footnote (c) times the references at L × 0.71 µs per execution, omitting the
   400 ns idle per layer that the delay-matched control adds by construction (Section 3b, Matched controls); the runner's model
   includes it (28.0 vs 23.6 min). The dial line still holds.
7. **"No masks" for the references** means every qubit idles 400 ns after every layer (the runner's `mask_p: 1` with
   `reset_kind: delay`); the pre-registration does not say whether "delay-matched p = 0" idles every qubit or only the qubits the
   paired p = 0.25 masks would reset (the smoke test's control does the latter, with shared masks). Both are logged; the
   references use the unmasked form, the contingent k = 1 control the shared-mask form.
8. **Dephasing dial mask probability.** "Z with probability p/2 per qubit per layer" at p = 0.5 is booked as `p: 0.5, mask_p:
   0.25`; the CSV `p` column carries 0.5.
9. **Characterisation.** The |+⟩ → reset → measure-X residual-coherence check and the rotation-survival identity at p > 0 have
   no Estimator circuit type; the reset-error P(1) map is in `dial_arm` (3 probes, 0.1 min of the 3-minute allowance). Paper 2's
   Q1 (f) measures the X-basis residual on the same device.
10. **Layer-index sweep k = 1 repeat.** "The k = 1 points repeat the main-grid point on the same day and double as a within-day
    repeat": booked with fresh draws in their own seed block so the rows are distinguishable from the main-grid point (the
    CSV carries no role column); an identical-θ repeat would need a role field in the logging schema.
11. **Job packing across points.** The pre-registration counts jobs per point (2 per grid point, 3 per reference, 2 per null
    control); the runner packs consecutive pubs of a group, so points straddle job boundaries and the counts differ (fewer
    jobs, less overhead). Harmless for the budget; relevant if a job fails (its pubs may belong to two points).
12. **Level-2 shots at n100, L = 8.** "16384 for the n = 100, L = 8 headline points" is read as the two main-grid points
    (Section 2's execution count for the level-2 grid, 5.9e7, gives 4096 for all eight level-2 points).
